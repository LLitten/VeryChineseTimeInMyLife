## Mistakes to watch

### Look-alike characters (write them side by side)
| Pair | Meanings | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| 少 / 小 | shǎo few / xiǎo small | 少 has an extra long slanting stroke at the bottom. |
| 下 / 不 | xià down / bù not | 不 has a slanting stroke (丿) under the top line; 下 doesn't. |
| 住 / 谁 | zhù to live / shéi who | Different left parts (亻 person vs 讠 speech) and different right parts. |
| 昨 / 作 | zuó (昨天) / zuò (工作) | 昨天 has the sun radical 日. Written as 作天 in one practice sentence. |
| 啤 / 脾 | pí (beer) / pí (spleen) | 啤酒 has the mouth radical 口, not the flesh radical 月. |
| 坐 / 做 | zuò sit / zuò do, make | Same sound. 坐 is two people on the ground. 做 has the person radical. |
| 字 / 子 | zì character / zi (儿子) | 字 is a child under a roof. |

### Meanings that get swapped
- **不客气 and 对不起:** 不客气 is "you're welcome," the reply to 谢谢. 对不起 is "sorry." 不好意思 is a lighter "excuse me."
- **漂亮 and 高兴:** pretty and happy.
- **菜 and 米饭:** dish or vegetables, and cooked rice.
- **和 and 也:** "and, with" (joins nouns) vs "also" (before a verb).
- **怎么样 and 怎么:** "how is it?" vs "how, why."
- **儿子 and 儿:** 儿子 is son; 儿 alone means child.
- **能 and 可能:** 能 is "can, be able to"; 可能 is "maybe."

### Words cut short or mistyped
- **电** for 电脑, **气** for 天气, **杯** for 杯子: the whole word is needed.
- **工死** for 工作 and **作天** for 昨天: when typing pinyin, check the candidate list before choosing.

### Grammar tendencies
- **English tense creeping in.** Mandarin has no tense; the time word does the work. 今天我不想去学校 is "I don't want to go today."
- **了 as past tense.** 了 marks completion or a change. In 他就要来了 it points to the future ("about to").
- **要 vs 在 for the future.** 在 + verb means happening now. For plans, use a time word, with or without 要.
- **不 vs 没.** Mostly chosen correctly now. 没 for the past, 不 for the future and habits. No 了 after 没 + verb, though 没钱了 ("out of money") is fine.
- **学 vs 看.** 学 is for subjects and skills (学中文). 看 is for reading (看书). The learner wrote 学书 twice.
- **都 and 也 placement.** After the subject and any time word, before the verb: 我们今天都在家.
- **Measure words.** 书 takes 本, not 个. After 没有, the number and measure word usually drop out.
- **Duration order.** Verb + duration + object: 学了两个小时数学.

### Regional and script notes
- **一点 vs 一点儿:** Taiwan usually says 一點 without 儿. Northern mainland speakers add 儿.
- **哪里 vs 哪儿:** 哪裡 is common in Taiwan, 哪儿 in the north.
- **Reading and writing:** simplified first. Traditional forms are recognition-only for now.
