## Part 1: A1 grammar points (AllSet order)

### Adverbs

#### 1. Negation of 有 with 没 ✅
- **Pattern:** 没 + 有 (+ noun)
- **Example:** 我没有书。 I don't have a book.
- **Note:** Never 不有. After 没有, the number and measure word usually drop out (not 我没有一本书).

#### 2. Standard negation with 不 ✅
- **Pattern:** 不 + verb / adjective
- **Use:** present, future, habits, and things you don't want to do.
- **Example:** 我不吃鱼。 I don't eat fish. 他不高。 He isn't tall.
- **Tone:** 不 is bù, but becomes bú before a 4th tone: 不是 bú shì, 不太 bú tài.

#### 3. The "all" adverb 都 ✅
- **Pattern:** Subject + 都 + verb / adjective
- **Example:** 我们都说英语。 We all speak English.
- **Note:** After the subject and any time word: 我们今天都在家.

#### 4. The "also" adverb 也 ✅
- **Pattern:** Subject + 也 + verb / adjective
- **Example:** 我也吃饭。 I'm eating too. 我也是。 Me too.
- **Note:** Never at the end of the sentence, unlike English "too."

### Conjunctions

#### 5. Expressing "and" with 和 ✅
- **Pattern:** Noun 1 + 和 + Noun 2
- **Example:** 我和朋友 my friend and I
- **Note:** 和 joins nouns only, not verbs or sentences. You typed 也 for 和 on the exam.

#### 6. Offering choices with 还是 ✅
- **Pattern:** A + 还是 + B ?
- **Example:** 你想喝咖啡还是茶？ Do you want coffee or tea?
- **Note:** 还是 is for questions. In statements use 或者: 我想喝咖啡或者茶. (或者 is in Part 2.)

### Numbers

#### 7. Age with 岁 ◐
- **Pattern:** Subject + number + 岁
- **Example:** 他女儿七岁。 His daughter is seven. *(your notes)*
- **Note:** No 是 needed. Ask with 几岁 (children) or 多大 (adults): 你多大了？

#### 8. Measure word 个 ✅
- **Pattern:** Number + 个 + noun
- **Example:** 三个朋友 three friends
- **Note:** A measure word is always needed between a number and a noun. 个 is the general one; many nouns have their own (书 takes 本). Your full measure-word list is in `vocab.csv`.

#### 9. Structure of dates ✅
- **Pattern:** Number + 年 + Number + 月 + Number + 日 / 号
- **Example:** 2026年11月9号。 November 9, 2026. 今天几月几号？ What's the date today?
- **Note:** Big to small: year, month, day. Addresses work the same way. 号 is spoken; 日 is written.

#### 10. Structure of days of the week ✅
- **Pattern:** 星期 + number
- **Example:** 星期一 Monday … 星期六 Saturday, 星期天 / 星期日 Sunday
- **Note:** Ask with 几: 今天星期几？

#### 11. Structure of numbers ✅
- **Pattern:** Number + unit (十, 百 …), big to small
- **Example:** 二十二 22, 一百 100

#### 12. Structure of times (basic) ✅
- **Pattern:** Number + 点 (钟)
- **Example:** 现在十点。 It's 10 o'clock. 两点 2 o'clock (not 二点).
- **Note:** Hour before minute: 十点二十分.

### Particles

#### 13. Expressing close possession without 的 ○
- **Pattern:** Pronoun + noun (family, close relationships, groups)
- **Example:** 我妈妈 my mom, 我朋友 my friend, 我们学校 our school
- **Note:** 我的妈妈 isn't wrong, just less natural for close relationships.

#### 14. Expressing possession with 的 ✅
- **Pattern:** Noun 1 + 的 + Noun 2
- **Example:** 我的书 my book, 老师的电脑 the teacher's computer
- **Note:** 的 also links a description to a noun; see Part 2.

#### 15. Questions with 呢 ◐
- **Pattern:** … 呢？ ("and …?", "what about …?")
- **Example:** 我很好，你呢？ I'm well, and you?
- **Note:** Your notes have 呢 at the end of other sentences (她们在买衣服呢, 爸爸什么时候来北京呢), where it softens the sentence or marks an ongoing action. This "and you?" use is the A1 one.

#### 16. Suggestions with 吧 ○
- **Pattern:** Command / suggestion + 吧
- **Example:** 走吧！ Let's go! 我们吃饭吧。 Let's eat.

### Verbs

#### 17. Expressing existence in a place with 在 ✅
- **Pattern:** Subject + 在 + place
- **Example:** 我在家。 I'm at home. 你在哪儿？ Where are you?
- **Note:** With an action, 在 + place comes before the verb: 我在家吃饭.

#### 18. Expressing existence with 有 ✅
- **Pattern:** Place + 有 + object
- **Example:** 桌子上有一个杯子。 There's a cup on the table. *(your notes)*

#### 19. Expressing possession with 有 ✅
- **Pattern:** Subject + 有 + object
- **Example:** 我有三个朋友。 I have three friends.

#### 20. Using the verb 叫 ○
- **Pattern:** Subject + 叫 + name
- **Example:** 我叫Sam。 My name is Sam. 你叫什么名字？ What's your name?

#### 21. Using the verb 去 ◐
- **Pattern:** Subject + 去 + place
- **Example:** 我明天去学校。 I'm going to school tomorrow.
- **Note:** No 在 is needed; 去 already carries the place.

#### 22. Using the verb 姓 ○
- **Pattern:** Subject + 姓 + surname
- **Example:** 我姓王。 My surname is Wang. 您贵姓？ What's your surname? (polite)

### Auxiliary verbs

#### 23. Expressing ability or possibility with 能 ◐
- **Pattern:** Subject + 能 + verb
- **Example:** 我能吃一块吗？ Can I have a piece?
- **Note:** 能 = able to (circumstances allow). 可能 = maybe. You answered "possible" for 能 on the exam.

#### 24. Expressing a learned skill with 会 ✅ ⚑
- **Pattern:** Subject + 会 + verb
- **Example:** 我会说中文。 I can speak Chinese. 我会画画。 I can draw. *(your notes)*
- ⚑ **Correction:** your note lists only "can (a learned skill)." 会 also predicts the future: 明天会很好 (tomorrow will be good).

#### 25. Expressing "be going to" with 要 ◐
- **Pattern:** Subject + 要 + verb (+ 了)
- **Example:** 我明天要去北京。 I'm going to Beijing tomorrow. 他就要来了。 He's about to arrive.
- **Note:** Don't use 在 for the future: 在 + verb means happening right now.

#### 26. Expressing permission with 可以 ○
- **Pattern:** Subject + 可以 + verb
- **Example:** 我可以坐这儿吗？ May I sit here? 可以。 Sure.

#### 27. Wanting to do something with 要 ◐
- **Pattern:** Subject + 要 + verb
- **Example:** 我要喝水。 I want water.
- **Related, in your notes:** 想 + verb is a softer "would like to": 我想买水. 喜欢 + verb is "like doing": 我喜欢吃饭.

### Verb phrases

#### 28. How to do something with 怎么 ◐
- **Pattern:** 怎么 + verb ？
- **Example:** 这个字怎么写？ How do you write this character? 这个怎么说？ How do you say this?
- **Note:** 怎么样 is different: "how is it?" (天气怎么样？).

#### 29. Negation of past actions with 没(有) ✅ ⚑
- **Pattern:** Subject + 没(有) + verb
- **Example:** 我昨天没吃饭。 I didn't eat yesterday.
- **Note:** Don't add 了 after the verb: ❌ 我没吃了.
- ⚑ **Correction:** your notes say "never use 了 with 没." That rule holds for 没 + verb. But 没 + noun + 了 is normal for "out of" or "no more": 没钱了 (I'm out of money), 没了 (it's gone).

#### 30. Negative commands with 不要 / 别 ✅
- **Pattern:** 不要 / 别 + verb
- **Example:** 别走！ Don't go! 别气我。 Don't make me mad. *(your notes)*
- **Note:** 别 and 不要 are mostly interchangeable. Adding 了 means "stop": 别说了 (stop talking).

### Basics

#### 31. Basic sentence order ✅
- **Pattern:** Subject + (time) + (place) + verb + object
- **Example:** 我明天在家吃饭。 I'll eat at home tomorrow.
- **Note:** Time can also open the sentence: 明天我在家吃饭. The full long-sentence order is in Part 2.

#### 32. Connecting nouns with 是 ✅
- **Pattern:** A + 是 + B
- **Example:** 我是学生。 I'm a student. 那个人是谁？ Who is that person? *(your notes)*
- **Note:** One word for is, am, are, was. Not used before adjectives (see 34).

#### 33. Expressing "excessively" with 太…了 ✅
- **Pattern:** 太 + adjective + 了
- **Example:** 太好了！ Great! 天气太热了。 It's too hot. *(your notes)*
- **Note:** The 了 is needed. For "not very," use 不太 with no 了 (Part 2).

#### 34. Simple "noun + adjective" sentences ✅
- **Pattern:** Noun + 很 + adjective
- **Example:** 他很高。 He is tall.
- **Note:** No 是 before an adjective. 很 often just links the subject and adjective, without meaning "very."

### Question forms

#### 35. Affirmative-negative questions ✅
- **Pattern:** Verb + 不 + verb / Adjective + 不 + adjective
- **Example:** 你是不是学生？ 你喜不喜欢？ 你有没有书？
- **Note:** Don't add 吗. With 有, use 有没有, not 有不有. Two-syllable verbs can shorten: 喜不喜欢.

#### 36. Placement of question words ✅
- **Pattern:** The question word takes the place of the missing information; word order doesn't change.
- **Example:** 你喜欢什么菜？ What food do you like? 你什么时候吃饭？ When do you eat?
- **Question words:** 什么 what, 谁 who, 哪儿 / 哪里 where, 哪个 which, 什么时候 when, 为什么 why, 怎么 how, 多少 / 几 how many

#### 37. Tag questions with 不 ✅
- **Pattern:** Statement, + 是不是 / 对不对 / 好不好 ？
- **Example:** 我们明天吃饭，好不好？ Let's eat tomorrow, okay?
- **Note:** Can't use 没 here.

#### 38. Tag questions with 吗 ○
- **Pattern:** Statement, + 是吗 / 对吗 / 好吗 ？
- **Example:** 你是老师，是吗？ You're a teacher, right? 我们走吧，好吗？ Let's go, okay?

#### 39. Yes-no questions with 吗 ✅
- **Pattern:** Statement + 吗 ？
- **Example:** 你喝水吗？ Do you drink water?
- **Answering:** yes, repeat the verb (喝。); no, negate it (不喝。).

### Comparisons

#### 40. Comparing 二 and 两 ✅
- **二** is the plain number: counting, 二十, 第二 (2nd), phone numbers.
- **两** comes before a measure word: 两个人, 两本书, 两点 (2 o'clock), 两块钱.

## Part 2: Beyond A1 (from your notes)

These are real patterns you've already met. Keep them, but focus on Part 1 first. Where AllSet places a pattern at a level, that level is given.

### Negation and degree
- **不太 + adjective** (AllSet HSK 1): not very. 我不太高兴. Pronounced bú tài, and no 了 at the end. *(your note wrote bù tài)*
- **A + 没有 + B + adjective:** A is not as … as B. 你没有我高 (you're not as tall as me).

### Actions in progress
- **(正)在 + verb (+ 呢)** (AllSet HSK 1): is doing. 她们在买衣服呢 (they're buying clothes). 正在 stresses "right now."

### Other verbs and frames
- **或者** (statements): or. 我想喝咖啡或者茶.
- **让 + person + verb:** let, make or ask someone. 他让我睡觉 (he lets me sleep). 你让我很高兴 (you make me happy).
- **想让 + person + verb:** want someone to. 我想让你学习.
- **得 + verb / 不用 + verb:** have to / don't need to. 我得走了. 你不用来. Not 不得.
- **Verbs that take a place after them:** 住在北京 (live in Beijing). 坐 takes a vehicle: 坐飞机.

### Complements
- **Verb + 到 (result):** you succeeded. 找到 find, 听到 hear, 看到 see, 买到 manage to buy. Negative: 找不到 (can't find).
- **Verb + 来 / 去 (direction):** toward (来) or away from (去) the speaker. 下来 come down, 下去 go down.

### Word order in longer sentences
- **Duration:** verb + duration + object. 我学了两个小时数学 (I studied math for two hours). ⚑ Your notes put the duration after the object.
- **Long-sentence order:** subject + time + manner + place + tool + with whom + verb + object (+ duration). 我昨天在学校用电脑跟朋友学数学. ⚑ Treat this as a guideline; the middle pieces shift in real speech.
- **Adverbs** go before the verb, after the subject: 我很喜欢这本书. ⚑ Your notes say adverbs can't come before the subject, but also that some can open a sentence. Both are partly right: most can't, but 也许 and 可能 can (可能他不来).
- **Noun order:** possessive, then this / that / number + measure word, then adjective, then noun: 我的两本新书.

### Describing
- **Description + 的 + noun:** 好吃的菜 (tasty food). Drop the noun to mean "the … one": 大的 (the big one).
- **Adjective + 地 + verb** (like -ly): 清楚地说 (speak clearly). The reduplicated form is 清清楚楚地. Not very common; plain adverbs don't need it.
- **"It" is usually dropped:** 现在五点 (it's 5 o'clock now). 下雨了 (it's raining).

## Corrections made to your notes

- **Duration:** your notes put the duration after the object. The usual order is verb + duration + object: 我学了两个小时数学.
- **会:** your notes list only "can (a learned skill)." It also means "will": 明天会很好.
- **什么时候:** your notes label it "future." It works for the past too: 你什么时候吃的？
- **Adverbs:** see Part 2. Most adverbs can't come before the subject; 也许 and 可能 can.
- **不太:** pronounced bú tài (the 4th-tone change), and it takes no 了.
- **Long-sentence order:** a guideline, not a fixed rule.
- **没 and 了:** the "never" rule applies to 没 + verb. 没钱了 ("out of money") is fine.
