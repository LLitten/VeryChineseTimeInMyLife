Sentence structure

Chinese word order starts like English (subject, verb, object) but differs once you add time, place or other details. This page shows where each piece goes, from short sentences to long ones.

1. Basic order

Level
A1 #31

Same order as English to start with: 我吃饭.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

Subject + verb (+ object)

Examples

  • 我去学校。wǒ qù xuéxiào.I go to school.
  • 他喝茶。tā hē chá.He drinks tea.

Notes

  • Verbs never change form. 是 is "is," "am," "are" and "was."
  • Things diverge from English once you add time, place or 也.

Practice

I eat.我吃饭。wǒ chīfàn.Say it, then tap to check

2. Time goes before the verb

Level
A2

Time can go first or right after the subject. Both are correct.

Structure Examples Watch out Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

Subject + time + verb, or time + subject + verb

Examples

  • 我明天吃饭。 / 明天我吃饭。wǒ míngtiān chīfàn. / míngtiān wǒ chīfàn.I'll eat tomorrow.
  • 他今天不去。tā jīntiān bú qù.He isn't going today.

Watch out

  • Not at the end, as in English: ✗ 我吃饭明天.

Practice

I'm going tomorrow.我明天去。wǒ míngtiān qù.Say it, then tap to check

3. Place goes after time, before the verb

Level
A1 #17
Keywords
在

在 + place comes before the action.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

Subject + time + 在 + place + verb + object

Examples

  • 我昨天在公司吃饭。wǒ zuótiān zài gōngsī chīfàn.I ate at work yesterday.
  • 我晚上在家看电视。wǒ wǎnshang zài jiā kàn diànshì.I watch TV at home in the evening.

Notes

  • Memory trick: you need time to get somewhere, and you need to be somewhere to do anything.

Practice

I study at school.我在学校学习。wǒ zài xuéxiào xuéxí.Say it, then tap to check

4. Verbs with 在 + place after them

Level
A2
Keywords
住、在、坐、放、站

A few verbs put 在 + place after the verb: 住 (live), 坐 (sit), 放 (put), 站 (stand).

Structure Examples Notes Watch out Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

住 / 坐 / 放 / 站 + 在 + place

Examples

  • 我住在北京。wǒ zhù zài Běijīng.I live in Beijing.
  • 你坐在这儿吧。nǐ zuò zài zhèr ba.Sit here.

Notes

  • 坐 can also take a vehicle directly: 坐飞机.

Watch out

  • Only these few. Don't say ✗ 我工作在公司.

Practice

Where do you live?你住在哪里？nǐ zhù zài nǎlǐ?Say it, then tap to check

5. How long: duration after the verb

Level
A2
Keywords
了

The time span goes after the verb and before the object.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

Subject + verb + 了 + duration + object

Examples

  • 我学了两个小时数学。wǒ xué le liǎng ge xiǎoshí shùxué.I studied math for two hours.
  • 他学了一年中文。tā xué le yì nián Zhōngwén.He studied Chinese for a year.AllSet
  • 我昨天学中文学了两个小时。wǒ zuótiān xué Zhōngwén xué le liǎng ge xiǎoshí.I studied Chinese for two hours yesterday.Yoyo

Notes

  • There's no word for "for" in "for two hours."
  • Or say the verb twice: verb + object + verb + 了 + duration: 我学数学学了两个小时.
  • 的 can go between the duration and the object: 学了两个小时的数学.

Practice

I slept for eight hours.我睡了八个小时。wǒ shuì le bā ge xiǎoshí.Say it, then tap to check

Sources

  • AllSet: Expressing duration with "le"
  • Yoyo Chinese: Grammar Series, Lesson 2 (Word Order 2)
  • Wording and unmarked examples: Claude

6. Putting it all together

Level
Beyond A1

A guideline for long sentences. The middle pieces shift in real speech.

Structure Examples Notes Sources Quiz me

Structure

Subject + time + manner + place + tool + with whom + verb + object

Examples

  • 我昨天在学校用电脑跟朋友学数学。wǒ zuótiān zài xuéxiào yòng diànnǎo gēn péngyou xué shùxué.Yesterday I studied math at school on a computer with my friend.
  • 我明天在图书馆和朋友一起学中文。wǒ míngtiān zài túshūguǎn hé péngyou yìqǐ xué Zhōngwén.Tomorrow I'll study Chinese at the library with a friend.Yoyo

Notes

  • Start with who and when, end with the action.
  • Yoyo's version: subject + when + where + how + action.

8. "It" is usually dropped

Level
Beyond A1

Chinese rarely has a word for "it." Say what you mean directly.

Examples Sources Quiz me

Examples

  • 现在五点。xiànzài wǔ diǎn.It's 5 o'clock.
  • 下雨了。xià yǔ le.It's raining.
  • 很好吃！hěn hǎochī!It's delicious!
  • 学中文很难。xué Zhōngwén hěn nán.It's hard to learn Chinese.Yoyo

9. 是 links two nouns

Level
A1 #32
Keywords
是

是 links two nouns: 我是学生.

Structure Examples Watch out Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

Noun 1 + 是 + noun 2

Examples

  • 我是学生。wǒ shì xuésheng.I'm a student.
  • 那个人是谁？nàge rén shì shéi?Who is that person?your notes

Watch out

  • Not used before an adjective: ✗ 他是高.

Practice

She's a teacher.她是老师。tā shì lǎoshī.Say it, then tap to check
