Actions in progress

To say something is happening right now, put 在 before the verb. This page shows how, and when to add 正在 or 呢.

1. (正)在 + verb: is doing

Level
A2
Keywords
呢、在、正

在 + verb = happening right now.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

Subject + (正)在 + verb (+ object) (+ 呢)

Examples

  • 她们在买衣服呢。tāmen zài mǎi yīfu ne.They're buying clothes.your notes
  • 我在喝茶。wǒ zài hē chá.I'm drinking tea.
  • 我们正在上课。wǒmen zhèngzài shàngkè.We're in class right now.AllSet

Notes

  • 正在 stresses "right now." Plain 在 is more common.
  • 呢 at the end adds a casual, ongoing feel.
  • Don't use it for plans. For the future, use a time word or 要.

Practice
