Art & drawing

These words help you talk about making art, whether it's a quick sketch or a finished illustration. Many art words share the character 画 (huà, draw, picture), which makes them easier to recognize.

Drawing and sketching

A 草图 is a rough first sketch; 素描 is a sketch or drawing study, often in pencil.

画huà畫ㄏㄨㄚˋto draw; a picture画画 = to draw (pictures).
画画huàhuà畫畫ㄏㄨㄚˋ ㄏㄨㄚˋto draw, paint
素描sùmiáo素描ㄙㄨˋ ㄇㄧㄠˊsketch; sketching
草图cǎotú草圖ㄘㄠˇ ㄊㄨˊrough sketch, draft
线条xiàntiáo線條ㄒㄧㄢˋ ㄊㄧㄠˊline, linework

Examples

  • 我喜欢画画。wǒ xǐhuan huàhuà.I like to draw.
  • 我先画草图。wǒ xiān huà cǎotú.I'll do a rough sketch first.

Color

上色 is "to color in," after the linework is done.

上色shàngsè上色ㄕㄤˋ ㄙㄜˋto color in
颜色yánsè顏色ㄧㄢˊ ㄙㄜˋcolor
颜料yánliào顏料ㄧㄢˊ ㄌㄧㄠˋpaint, pigment
水彩shuǐcǎi水彩ㄕㄨㄟˇ ㄘㄞˇwatercolor

Examples

  • 我还没上色。wǒ hái méi shàngsè.I haven't colored it yet.
  • 我喜欢用水彩。wǒ xǐhuan yòng shuǐcǎi.I like using watercolor.

Art in general

艺术 is "art" in the broad sense; 美术 is visual art, and also the art team at a game studio.

艺术yìshù藝術ㄧˋ ㄕㄨˋart
美术měishù美術ㄇㄟˇ ㄕㄨˋart; the art team游戏美术 = game artist.
画家huàjiā畫家ㄏㄨㄚˋ ㄐㄧㄚpainter, artist
作品zuòpǐn作品ㄗㄨㄛˋ ㄆㄧㄣˇa work, piece (of art)
设计shèjì設計ㄕㄜˋ ㄐㄧˋdesign; to design

Examples

  • 这是我的作品。zhè shì wǒ de zuòpǐn.This is my work.
  • 他是画家。tā shì huàjiā.He's a painter.

Pens, pencils and paper

笔 is any pen or brush; 铅笔 ("lead pen") is a pencil.

笔bǐ筆ㄅㄧˇpen, brush
铅笔qiānbǐ鉛筆ㄑㄧㄢ ㄅㄧˇpencil
纸zhǐ紙ㄓˇpaper
电脑diànnǎo電腦ㄉㄧㄢˋ ㄋㄠˇcomputer

Examples

  • 我用铅笔画素描。wǒ yòng qiānbǐ huà sùmiáo.I sketch with a pencil.
  • 我在电脑上画画。wǒ zài diànnǎo shàng huàhuà.I draw on the computer.

Practice

I draw on the computer.我在电脑上画画。wǒ zài diànnǎo shàng huàhuà.Say it, then tap to check
