I, you, he, she

他 and 她 sound the same; only the writing differs.

我wǒ我ㄨㄛˇI, me
你nǐ你ㄋㄧˇyou
他tā他ㄊㄚhe
她tā她ㄊㄚsheSounds the same as 他; only the written character differs.
们men們˙ㄇㄣplural marker for peoplePlural for people: 我们, 你们, 他们.
我们wǒmen我們ㄨㄛˇ ˙ㄇㄣwe
她们tāmen她們ㄊㄚ ˙ㄇㄣthey (female)

Examples

  • 我们是朋友。wǒmen shì péngyou.We're friends.

This and that

Before a noun they need a measure word: 这个, 那本.

这zhè這ㄓㄜˋthiszhè; often zhèi before a measure word: 这个.
那nà那ㄋㄚˋthatnà; often nèi before a measure word: 那个.

Examples

  • 这是我的，那是你的。zhè shì wǒ de, nà shì nǐ de.This is mine; that's yours.

Practice

They're students.他们是学生。tāmen shì xuésheng.Say it, then tap to check

One to ten, and beyond

Numbers build up regularly: 十一 (11), 二十 (20), 二十二 (22).

零líng零ㄌㄧㄥˊzero (0)
一yī一ㄧone (1)
二èr二ㄦˋtwo (2)Used in 20, 22 and “2nd.”
三sān三ㄙㄢthree (3)
四sì四ㄙˋfour (4)
五wǔ五ㄨˇfive (5)
六liù六ㄌㄧㄡˋsix (6)
七qī七ㄑㄧseven (7)
八bā八ㄅㄚeight (8)
九jiǔ九ㄐㄧㄡˇnine (9)
十shí十ㄕˊten (10)
百bǎi百ㄅㄞˇhundred (100)

Examples

  • 三十五sānshíwǔ35

二 or 两

二 is for counting and numbers. 两 goes before a measure word: 两个人.

两liǎng兩ㄌㄧㄤˇtwo (counting things)Used before measure words and for money amounts: 两个人.

Examples

  • 我有两个朋友。wǒ yǒu liǎng ge péngyou.I have two friends.

Tone changes in 一

一 is yī alone, yí before a 4th tone (一个 yí ge), and yì before the others (一起 yìqǐ).

Practice

two books两本书liǎng běn shūSay it, then tap to check

The everyday ones

个 works for most things when you're not sure.

个gè個ㄍㄜˋgeneral measure wordPeople and objects in general.
些xiē些ㄒㄧㄝsome, a few多吃些水果.

Examples

  • 我要一些水果。wǒ yào yìxiē shuǐguǒ.I'd like some fruit.

For people

位 is the polite one.

位wèi位ㄨㄟˋpeople (respectful)e.g. a teacher.
名míng名ㄇㄧㄥˊpeople with a status or jobe.g. a doctor.
口kǒu口ㄎㄡˇfamily members, householdsRarely spoken: a family of four.

Examples

  • 一位老师yí wèi lǎoshīa teacher (polite)

Very, too and not very

不太 (not very) takes no 了.

很hěn很ㄏㄣˇveryOften just links a subject and an adjective.
非常fēicháng非常ㄈㄟ ㄔㄤˊextremely
太tài太ㄊㄞˋtoo, so太…了 needs 了.
不太bú tài不太ㄅㄨˊ ㄊㄞˋnot veryPronounced bú tài (the 4th-tone change). No 了.
一点儿yìdiǎnr一點兒ㄧ ㄉㄧㄢˇㄦa little bitTaiwan usually says 一點 without 儿.

Examples

  • 这个太大了。zhège tài dà le.This is too big.
  • 我不太高兴。wǒ bú tài gāoxìng.I'm not very happy.

Common describing words

好hǎo好ㄏㄠˇgood

少shǎo少ㄕㄠˇfew, littleLook-alike: 小 (small). 少 has the extra slanting stroke.
漂亮piàoliang漂亮ㄆㄧㄠˋ ˙ㄌㄧㄤpretty
高兴gāoxìng高興ㄍㄠ ㄒㄧㄥˋhappy
清楚qīngchu清楚ㄑㄧㄥ ˙ㄔㄨclear

Examples

Linking words

的de的˙ㄉㄜ’s; links a description to a noun
地de地˙ㄉㄜadverb marker (like -ly)Not super common. 清楚地 = clearly.
和hé和ㄏㄜˊand (nouns only)Doesn’t join verbs or sentences.
还是háishì還是ㄏㄞˊ ㄕˋor (in questions)
或者huòzhě或者ㄏㄨㄛˋ ㄓㄜˇor (in statements)

Examples

  • 我和朋友wǒ hé péngyoumy friend and I

Saying no

不 is for now, the future and habits. 没 is for the past and for 有. 别 / 不要 mean "don't."

不bù不ㄅㄨˋnotBecomes bú before a 4th tone: 不是 bú shì.
没méi沒ㄇㄟˊnot; didn’tPast or not-yet actions, and the negative of 有. Never add 了.
没有méiyǒu沒有ㄇㄟˊ ㄧㄡˇdon’t have; didn’t有 is optional in some phrases: 我没办法.
别bié別ㄅㄧㄝˊdon’t
不要búyào不要ㄅㄨˊ ㄧㄠˋdon’t别 and 不要 are mostly interchangeable.
不用búyòng不用ㄅㄨˊ ㄩㄥˋdon’t need to

Examples

  • 我昨天没去。wǒ zuótiān méi qù.I didn't go yesterday.

Also, all, and other helpers

也 and 都 go before the verb, never at the end. 了 marks a completed action or a change.

也yě也ㄧㄝˇalso, tooAlways after the subject; never at the end.
都dōu都ㄉㄡall, both
也都yědōu也都ㄧㄝˇ ㄉㄡalso all / everyone也 and 都 together: 我们也都去 (we’re all going too).
了le了˙ㄌㄜcompletion / change particleMarks a completed action or a change.
是shì是ㄕˋto beOne word for is, was, am, are.
有yǒu有ㄧㄡˇto have; there is/are
在zài在ㄗㄞˋto be at, be in; doing
会huì會ㄏㄨㄟˋcan (a learned skill); willAlso means “will”: 明天会很好 (tomorrow will be good).
能néng能ㄋㄥˊcan, be able to
得děi得ㄉㄟˇhave to, mustNot for “don’t have to”: use 不用.
让ràng讓ㄖㄤˋto let, allow, make, ask
用yòng用ㄩㄥˋto use; with (a tool)
到dào到ㄉㄠˋto arrive; result marker

Examples

  • 我们都会说英语。wǒmen dōu huì shuō Yīngyǔ.We can all speak English.
