Conversation

Once you've said hello, these words keep the conversation going. You'll learn quick reactions, ways to encourage a friend, how to give an opinion and ask questions, and what to say when you don't understand.

1. Reacting

Used for
Reacting & agreeingMaking plans
Keywords
不错、你呢、可以、好吃、好啊、好的、对啊、当然

Short reactions keep a conversation going and show you're listening.

Agreeing Surprise Praise and modesty Going along with a plan Practice Sources Quiz me

Agreeing

确实 agrees that what someone just said is true.

对啊duì a對啊ㄉㄨㄟˋ ˙ㄚyeah, right啊 softens it. Very common in everyday speech.
确实quèshí確實ㄑㄩㄝˋ ㄕˊthat’s right, indeedUsed to agree with what someone just said.from Koa
当然dāngrán當然ㄉㄤ ㄖㄢˊof course
好的hǎo de好的ㄏㄠˇ ˙ㄉㄜokay
我也是wǒ yě shì我也是ㄨㄛˇ ㄧㄝˇ ㄕˋme too

Examples

  • 今天好热！ 对啊！jīntiān hǎo rè! duì a!It's so hot today! Yeah!
  • 我也是。wǒ yě shì.Me too.

Surprise

From mild to strong: 是吗？ → 真的吗？ → 真的假的？

是吗？shì ma?是嗎？ㄕˋ ˙ㄇㄚ？oh really? is that so?Milder than 真的吗？
真的吗？zhēn de ma?真的嗎？ㄓㄣ ˙ㄉㄜ ˙ㄇㄚ？really?
真的假的？zhēn de jiǎ de?真的假的？ㄓㄣ ˙ㄉㄜ ㄐㄧㄚˇ ˙ㄉㄜ？no way! seriously?Casual and a little dramatic. Common in Taiwan.
然后呢？ránhòu ne?然後呢？ㄖㄢˊ ㄏㄡˋ ˙ㄋㄜ？and then?Shows you're listening and want to hear more.

Examples

  • 我明天去台北。 真的吗？wǒ míngtiān qù Táiběi. zhēn de ma?I'm going to Taipei tomorrow. Really?
  • 然后呢？ránhòu ne?And then?

Praise and modesty

Friends usually brush praise off. 没有啦 is common in Taiwan.

好厉害！hǎo lìhai!好厲害！ㄏㄠˇ ㄌㄧˋ ˙ㄏㄞ！wow, impressive!Very common praise between friends.
不错búcuò不錯ㄅㄨˊ ㄘㄨㄛˋnot bad, pretty goodMilder than 好.from a YouTube video
太棒了！tài bàng le!太棒了！ㄊㄞˋ ㄅㄤˋ ˙ㄌㄜ！awesome!
好吃hǎochī好吃ㄏㄠˇ ㄔdelicious (food)
没有啦méiyǒu la沒有啦ㄇㄟˊ ㄧㄡˇ ˙ㄌㄚoh, not really (modest reply to praise)A modest reply. Common in Taiwan.

Examples

  • 你的中文很好！ 没有啦！nǐ de Zhōngwén hěn hǎo! méiyǒu la!Your Chinese is great! Oh, not really!
  • 好厉害！hǎo lìhai!Wow, impressive!

Going along with a plan

随便 (up to you) can sound like you don't care.

好啊hǎo a好啊ㄏㄠˇ ˙ㄚsure, sounds goodA friendly yes to an invitation.
可以kěyǐ可以ㄎㄜˇ ㄧˇokay, that works; can (allowed)
随便suíbiàn隨便ㄙㄨㄟˊ ㄅㄧㄢˋwhatever, up to youFine with friends, but it can sound like you don't care.
你呢？nǐ ne?你呢？ㄋㄧˇ ˙ㄋㄜ？and you?Turns the same question back to the other person.

Examples

  • 你想吃什么？ 随便，你呢？nǐ xiǎng chī shénme? suíbiàn, nǐ ne?What do you want to eat? Up to you. You?

Practice

Me too.我也是。wǒ yě shì.Say it, then tap to check
Really?真的吗？zhēn de ma?Say it, then tap to check
And then?然后呢？ránhòu ne?Say it, then tap to check

Checking on someone

怎么了？zěnme le?怎麼了？ㄗㄣˇ ˙ㄇㄜ ˙ㄌㄜ？what's wrong? what happened?Asks a friend who seems upset.
没事méi shì沒事ㄇㄟˊ ㄕˋit's nothing, I'm fine, no problemAlso a casual reply to 对不起 or 谢谢.
小心xiǎoxīn小心ㄒㄧㄠˇ ㄒㄧㄣbe careful

Examples

  • 你怎么了？ 没事，我很好。nǐ zěnme le? méi shì, wǒ hěn hǎo.What's wrong? Nothing, I'm fine.
  • 小心！xiǎoxīn!Careful!

Cheering on

加油 is for before something hard; 恭喜 is for after it goes well.

加油jiāyóu加油ㄐㄧㄚ ㄧㄡˊyou can do it! go for it!
你可以的！nǐ kěyǐ de!你可以的！ㄋㄧˇ ㄎㄜˇ ㄧˇ ˙ㄉㄜ！you can do it!Like 加油, for encouragement.
恭喜gōngxǐ恭喜ㄍㄨㄥ ㄒㄧˇcongratulations
辛苦了xīnkǔ le辛苦了ㄒㄧㄣ ㄎㄨˇ ˙ㄌㄜthanks for your hard workSaid to someone who just did a lot of work.

Examples

  • 明天考试，加油！míngtiān kǎoshì, jiāyóu!Exam tomorrow. You've got this!
  • 恭喜你！gōngxǐ nǐ!Congratulations!
  • 辛苦了！xīnkǔ le!Thanks for all your hard work!

Reassuring

没问题 works for agreeing to help and for saying something's fine.

别担心bié dānxīn別擔心ㄅㄧㄝˊ ㄉㄢ ㄒㄧㄣdon't worry
没问题méi wèntí沒問題ㄇㄟˊ ㄨㄣˋ ㄊㄧˊno problem

Examples

  • 别担心，没问题！bié dānxīn, méi wèntí!Don't worry, no problem!

Practice

Go for it!加油！jiāyóu!Say it, then tap to check

These words say what you like, want or think. Feelings have their own lesson.

Liking and wanting

Both take a verb after them. 想 is softer than 要.

喜欢xǐhuan喜歡ㄒㄧˇ ˙ㄏㄨㄢto likeCan shorten: 喜不喜欢？
想xiǎng想ㄒㄧㄤˇto want to
爱ài愛ㄞˋto love

Examples

  • 我喜欢喝茶。wǒ xǐhuan hē chá.I like drinking tea.
  • 我想喝咖啡。wǒ xiǎng hē kāfēi.I'd like a coffee.

Thinking and knowing

知道 is knowing a fact; 认识 is knowing a person.

觉得juéde覺得ㄐㄩㄝˊ ˙ㄉㄜto think, feel
你觉得…？nǐ juéde…?你覺得…？ㄋㄧˇ ㄐㄩㄝˊ ˙ㄉㄜ…？what do you think? / do you think…?from Koa
知道zhīdào知道ㄓ ㄉㄠˋto know (a fact)
怎么样zěnmeyàng怎麼樣ㄗㄣˇ ˙ㄇㄜ ㄧㄤˋhow is it? what kind?Seen in 天气怎么样？ (how’s the weather?)

Examples

  • 你觉得这个怎么样？ 我觉得很好吃！nǐ juéde zhège zěnmeyàng? wǒ juéde hěn hǎochī!What do you think of this? I think it's delicious!
  • 我不知道。wǒ bù zhīdào.I don't know.

Maybe

Both can start a sentence.

可能kěnéng可能ㄎㄜˇ ㄋㄥˊmaybe, possible
也许yěxǔ也許ㄧㄝˇ ㄒㄩˇmaybe
高兴gāoxìng高興ㄍㄠ ㄒㄧㄥˋhappy

Examples

  • 可能他不来。kěnéng tā bù lái.Maybe he isn't coming.

Practice

I'd like to drink coffee.我想喝咖啡。wǒ xiǎng hē kāfēi.Say it, then tap to check
What do you think?你觉得呢？nǐ juéde ne?Say it, then tap to check

Who, what, which

谁shéi誰ㄕㄟˊwho
什么shénme什麼ㄕㄣˊ ˙ㄇㄜwhat
哪个nǎge哪個ㄋㄚˇ ˙ㄍㄜwhich
哪nǎ哪ㄋㄚˇwhichnǎ; often něi before a measure word: 哪个.

Examples

  • 他是谁？tā shì shéi?Who is he?
  • 你喜欢什么菜？nǐ xǐhuan shénme cài?What food do you like?

Where, when, why, how

哪里 / 哪儿nǎlǐ / nǎr哪裡 / 哪兒ㄋㄚˇ ㄌㄧˇ / ㄋㄚˇㄦwhere
什么时候shénme shíhou什麼時候ㄕㄣˊ ˙ㄇㄜ ㄕˊ ˙ㄏㄡwhenPast or future: 你什么时候吃的？
为什么wèishénme為什麼ㄨㄟˋ ㄕㄣˊ ˙ㄇㄜwhy
怎么zěnme怎麼ㄗㄣˇ ˙ㄇㄜhow

Examples

  • 你什么时候来？nǐ shénme shíhou lái?When are you coming?
  • 你为什么不吃？nǐ wèishénme bù chī?Why aren't you eating?

How many, how much

几 is for small numbers; 多少 is for any amount.

几jǐ幾ㄐㄧˇhow many (small numbers)Often used for weekdays, dates and times.
多少duōshao多少ㄉㄨㄛ ˙ㄕㄠhow much, how many
多duō多ㄉㄨㄛhow (degree); many, much多 + adjective asks “how …”: 你多高？

Examples

  • 这个多少钱？zhège duōshao qián?How much is this?

Yes/no questions

呢ne呢˙ㄋㄜsentence-final particleSeen at the end of 她们在买衣服呢 and 爸爸什么时候来北京呢.
你喜欢做什么？nǐ xǐhuan zuò shénme?你喜歡做什麼？ㄋㄧˇ ㄒㄧˇ ˙ㄏㄨㄢ ㄗㄨㄛˋ ㄕㄣˊ ˙ㄇㄜ？what do you like to do?

Examples

  • 你喜欢做什么？nǐ xǐhuan zuò shénme?What do you like to do?

Practice

Saying you don't understand

听不懂tīng bu dǒng聽不懂ㄊㄧㄥ ˙ㄅㄨ ㄉㄨㄥˇI don't understand (what I heard)Same frame as 找不到.

Asking for help

请再说一次qǐng zài shuō yí cì請再說一次ㄑㄧㄥˇ ㄗㄞˋ ㄕㄨㄛ ㄧˊ ㄘˋplease say it again

说shuō說ㄕㄨㄛto speak, say

Examples

  • 这个用中文怎么说？zhège yòng Zhōngwén zěnme shuō?How do you say this in Chinese?

中文zhōngwén中文ㄓㄨㄥ ㄨㄣˊChinese language (common word)
汉语Hànyǔ漢語ㄏㄢˋ ㄩˇChinese language
英语Yīngyǔ英語ㄧㄥ ㄩˇEnglish
文wén文ㄨㄣˊlanguage, writingMostly inside words: 中文, 英文.

Thanks and sorry

Each has a standard reply: 谢谢 → 不客气, 对不起 → 没关系.

谢谢xièxie謝謝ㄒㄧㄝˋ ˙ㄒㄧㄝthank you
不客气bú kèqi不客氣ㄅㄨˊ ㄎㄜˋ ˙ㄑㄧyou’re welcomeThe reply to 谢谢.
对不起duìbuqǐ對不起ㄉㄨㄟˋ ˙ㄅㄨ ㄑㄧˇsorryA real apology. 不好意思 is lighter (excuse me).
没关系méi guānxi沒關係ㄇㄟˊ ㄍㄨㄢ ˙ㄒㄧit doesn’t matterReply to 对不起.
不好意思bù hǎoyìsi不好意思ㄅㄨˋ ㄏㄠˇ ㄧˋ ˙ㄙexcuse me; sorry (light)For small things; 对不起 is a real apology.

Examples

  • 谢谢！ 不客气。xièxie! bú kèqi.Thanks! You're welcome.

Please and wait

请 + verb = please …: 请坐.

请qǐng請ㄑㄧㄥˇplease, to invite
等一下děng yíxià等一下ㄉㄥˇ ㄧˊ ㄒㄧㄚˋwait a moment一下 softens a verb: “a bit.”

Examples

  • 不好意思，等一下。bù hǎoyìsi, děng yíxià.Sorry, one moment.

Practice
