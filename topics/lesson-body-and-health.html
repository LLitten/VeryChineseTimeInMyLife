2. Saying what hurts

Used for
Health & bodyFeelings & opinions
Keywords
疼、累、舒服

Body part + 疼 = it hurts: 头疼 (headache), 肚子疼 (stomachache). In Taiwan, 痛 (tòng) is more common: 頭痛.

Pain Feeling unwell Practice Sources

Pain

疼téng疼ㄊㄥˊto hurt, acheTaiwan often says 痛 (tòng): 頭痛.

Examples

  • 我头疼。wǒ tóu téng.I have a headache.
  • 我肚子疼。wǒ dùzi téng.My stomach hurts.

Feeling unwell

舒服 is "comfortable." 不舒服 is the usual way to say you feel unwell. 累 is "tired."

舒服shūfu舒服ㄕㄨ ˙ㄈㄨcomfortable不舒服 = unwell.
累lèi累ㄌㄟˋtired

Examples

  • 我不舒服。wǒ bù shūfu.I don't feel well.
  • 我今天很累。wǒ jīntiān hěn lèi.I'm really tired today.

Practice

I have a headache.我头疼。wǒ tóu téng.Say it, then tap to check
I don't feel well.我不舒服。wǒ bù shūfu.Say it, then tap to check

Being sick Getting better Staying healthy Practice Sources

Being sick

生病 is "get sick." 感冒 is a cold; 我感冒了 means "I've caught a cold."

生病shēngbìng生病ㄕㄥ ㄅㄧㄥˋto get sick
感冒gǎnmào感冒ㄍㄢˇ ㄇㄠˋa cold; to catch a cold

Examples

  • 我感冒了。wǒ gǎnmào le.I've got a cold.
  • 他生病了。tā shēngbìng le.He's sick.

Getting better

You 吃药 (eat medicine) to take medicine, and 看医生 to see a doctor.

药yào藥ㄧㄠˋmedicine吃药 = take medicine.
看医生kàn yīshēng看醫生ㄎㄢˋ ㄧ ㄕㄥto see a doctor
医生yīshēng醫生ㄧ ㄕㄥdoctor
医院yīyuàn醫院ㄧ ㄩㄢˋhospital

Examples

  • 你要吃药。nǐ yào chī yào.You need to take some medicine.
  • 我明天去看医生。wǒ míngtiān qù kàn yīshēng.I'm seeing a doctor tomorrow.

Staying healthy

运动 is "exercise" or "sports."

运动yùndòng運動ㄩㄣˋ ㄉㄨㄥˋsports, exercise

Examples

  • 我每天运动。wǒ měi tiān yùndòng.I exercise every day.

Practice

I've caught a cold.我感冒了。wǒ gǎnmào le.Say it, then tap to check
Take some medicine.吃药吧。chī yào ba.Say it, then tap to check
