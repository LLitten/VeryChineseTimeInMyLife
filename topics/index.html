Grammar

Checked against the AllSet Chinese Grammar Wiki. Levels are AllSet's.

1. Sentence structureThe order words go in: subject, time, place, verb, object.Basic order · Time goes before the verb · Place goes after time, before the verb · Verbs with 在 + place after them · How long: duration after the verb · Putting it all together · Order inside a noun phrase · "It" is usually dropped · 是 links two nouns
2. Yes / no and other questions吗, verb-not-verb, tag questions, question words, 呢 and 吧.Yes-no questions with 吗 · Verb + 不 + verb · Tag questions: 好不好, 对不对 · Tag questions: 好吗, 对吗 · Question words stay in place · How to do something: 怎么 · "And you?" with 呢 · Suggestions with 吧
3. Negation不, 没, 别, 不太 and "not as … as."不 + verb / adjective · 没(有) + verb: didn't · 没有 + noun: don't have · Don't: 别 / 不要 · 不太 + adjective: not very · Not as … as: 没有
4. Adverbs and describing很, 太…了, 都, 也, where adverbs go, 地 and 多 + adjective.Noun + 很 + adjective · 太 + adjective + 了 · 都: all, both · 也: also, too · Where adverbs go · Adjective + 地 + verb · How …? with 多 + adjective
5. Linking words和, 还是 and 或者, and the two jobs of 的.和: and, with · Or: 还是 and 或者 · 的: possession · Close people: no 的 · 的: description + noun
6. Verbs and helping verbsWant, can, may, have to, let, and verbs for names and going places.Want: 要, 想, 喜欢 · Going to: 要 · Can: 会 and 能 · May: 可以 · Have to: 得 · Don't need to: 不用 · Let or make someone: 让 · Going places: 去 · Names: 叫 and 姓
7. Complements (what happens after the verb)Verb + 到 for results, and verb + 来 / 去 for direction.Verb + 到: you got the result · Verb + 来 / 去: which direction
8. Actions in progressSaying something is happening right now with 在 and 正在.(正)在 + verb: is doing
9. Numbers, measure words, dates and timeBuilding numbers, 二 vs 两, measure words, age, dates, weekdays and clock time.Building numbers · 二 vs 两 · Measure words · Age with 岁 · Dates · Days of the week · Clock time
10. Location and "there is"在 for where things are, 有 for "there is" and "have", and direction words.Be somewhere: 在 · There is: place + 有 · Have: subject + 有 · Exactly where: direction words
