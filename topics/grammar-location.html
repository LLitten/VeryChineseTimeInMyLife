Location and "there is"

在 for where things are, 有 for "there is" and "have", and direction words.

  • Be somewhere: 在
  • There is: place + 有
  • Have: subject + 有
  • Exactly where: direction words

Be somewhere: 在 A1 #17

Subject + 在 + place

How it works

  • 在 is the verb here. Don't add 是: ❌ 我是在家.
  • Negate with 不: 他不在学校.

Examples

我在家。wǒ zài jiā.I'm at home.
你在哪儿？nǐ zài nǎr?Where are you?

Try it

He isn't at school.他不在学校。tā bú zài xuéxiào.Say it, then tap to check

There is: place + 有 A1 #18

Place + 有 + thing

How it works

  • The place goes first: "the table has a cup" = there's a cup on the table.
  • Negative: 没有.

Examples

桌子上有一个杯子。zhuōzi shàng yǒu yí ge bēizi.There's a cup on the table.your notes
附近有公园吗？fùjìn yǒu gōngyuán ma?Is there a park nearby?

Try it

There are people in the room.房间里有人。fángjiān lǐ yǒu rén.Say it, then tap to check

Have: subject + 有 A1 #19

Subject + 有 + thing

How it works

  • "Have." Negative: 没有, never 不有.

Examples

我有三个朋友。wǒ yǒu sān ge péngyou.I have three friends.
我有钱。wǒ yǒu qián.I have money.AllSet

Try it

I have a dog.我有一只狗。wǒ yǒu yì zhī gǒu.Say it, then tap to check

Exactly where: direction words A2

Subject + 在 + place + direction word

How it works

  • The direction word goes after the place: 学校后边 (behind the school).
  • Short 上 and 里 attach straight to the noun: 桌子上, 房间里.

Examples

猫在桌子上面。māo zài zhuōzi shàngmiàn.The cat is on the table.
商店在学校后边。shāngdiàn zài xuéxiào hòubiān.The shop is behind the school.

Try it

The bank is next to the school.银行在学校旁边。yínháng zài xuéxiào pángbiān.Say it, then tap to check
