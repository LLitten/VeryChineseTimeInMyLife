Yes / no and other questions

Grammar section 2 of 10. Tags show the AllSet level.

Yes-no questions with 吗 A1 #39

Statement + 吗 ？ Word order stays the same.

你喝水吗？ Do you drink water?

Verb + 不 + verb A1 #35

Verb + 不 + verb / Adjective + 不 + adjective

你是不是学生？ 你喜不喜欢？

  • Note: Don't add 吗. Two-syllable verbs can shorten: 喜不喜欢. With two verbs, use the main (usually first) one.

有没有 A1 #35

有没有 + noun

你有没有书？ Do you have a book?

  • Note: Never 有不有, because the negative of 有 is 没有.

Answering yes / no A1 #39

Yes: repeat the verb. 你喝水吗？ 喝。

  • No: negate the verb. 不喝。

Tag questions with 不 A1 #37

Statement, + 好不好 / 是不是 / 对不对 ？

我们明天吃饭，好不好？ Let's eat tomorrow, okay?

  • Note: Can't use 没 here.

Tag questions with 吗 A1 #38

Statement, + 好吗 / 是吗 / 对吗 ？

你是老师，是吗？ You're a teacher, right?

Question words stay in place A1 #36

the question word takes the place of the missing information; word order doesn't change.

你喜欢什么菜？ What food do you like? 你什么时候吃饭？ When do you eat?

  • Question words: 什么 what, 谁 who, 哪儿 / 哪里 where, 哪个 which, 什么时候 when, 为什么 why, 怎么 how, 多少 / 几 how many

How to do something with 怎么 A1 #28

怎么 + verb ？

这个字怎么写？ How do you write this character?

  • Note: 怎么样 is different: "how is it?" (天气怎么样？).

"And you?" with 呢 A1 #15

… 呢？

我很好，你呢？ I'm well, and you?

  • Note: 呢 also ends other sentences, where it softens the sentence or marks an ongoing action: 她们在买衣服呢, 爸爸什么时候来北京呢.

Suggestions with 吧 A1 #16

Suggestion + 吧

走吧！ Let's go! 我们吃饭吧。 Let's eat.
