Yes / no and other questions

1. Yes-no questions with 吗

Level
A1 #39
Keywords
吗

Add 吗 to the end of any statement. Word order stays the same.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

Statement + 吗？

Examples

  • 你喝水吗？ 喝。 / 不喝。nǐ hē shuǐ ma? hē. / bù hē.Do you drink water? Yes. / No.
  • 他是老师吗？tā shì lǎoshī ma?Is he a teacher?

Notes

  • Don't add 吗 if there's already a question word or 是不是.
  • Answer by repeating the verb (喝 / 不喝), or with 对 (right).
  • For the past: 你去了吗？ → 去了 (yes) / 没去 (no).

Practice

Are you a student?你是学生吗？nǐ shì xuésheng ma?Say it, then tap to check

2. Verb + 不 + verb

Level
A1 #35
Keywords
不

Offers both choices: 去不去 = going or not?

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

Verb + 不 + verb / adjective + 不 + adjective

Examples

  • 你是不是学生？nǐ shì bu shì xuésheng?Are you a student?
  • 你喜不喜欢咖啡？nǐ xǐ bu xǐhuan kāfēi?Do you like coffee?
  • 你有没有钱？nǐ yǒu méiyǒu qián?Do you have money?

Notes

  • Don't add 吗.
  • Two-syllable words can shorten: 喜欢不喜欢 or 喜不喜欢. Both are fine.
  • For 有, use 有没有: 你有没有书？

Practice

Are you going?你去不去？nǐ qù bu qù?Say it, then tap to check

3. Tag questions: 好不好, 对不对

Level
A1 #37
Keywords
好不好、对不对、是不是

Asks for agreement, like "okay?" or "right?"

Structure Examples Notes Sources Quiz me

Structure

Statement, + 好不好 / 对不对 / 是不是？

Examples

  • 我们明天吃饭，好不好？wǒmen míngtiān chīfàn, hǎo bu hǎo?Let's eat tomorrow, okay?
  • 你会说中文，对不对？nǐ huì shuō Zhōngwén, duì bu duì?You speak Chinese, right?

Notes

  • Can't use 没 here.

4. Tag questions: 好吗, 对吗

Level
A1 #38
Keywords
好吗、对吗、是吗、可以吗

Softer ways to check or ask: 好吗 (okay?), 对吗 (right?), 是吗 (really?).

Structure Examples Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

Statement, + 好吗 / 对吗 / 是吗 / 可以吗？

Examples

  • 你是老师，是吗？nǐ shì lǎoshī, shì ma?You're a teacher, right?
  • 我们走吧，好吗？wǒmen zǒu ba, hǎo ma?Let's go, okay?

5. Question words stay in place

Level
A1 #36

Nothing moves, unlike English "what/where" questions.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

The question word goes where the answer would be

Examples

  • 你喜欢什么菜？nǐ xǐhuan shénme cài?What food do you like?
  • 你在哪里？nǐ zài nǎlǐ?Where are you?
  • 你什么时候吃饭？nǐ shénme shíhou chīfàn?When do you eat?

Notes

  • 什么 what · 谁 who · 哪儿 / 哪里 where · 哪个 which · 什么时候 when · 为什么 why · 怎么 how · 多少 / 几 how many.

Practice

Who is he?他是谁？tā shì shéi?Say it, then tap to check

6. How to do something: 怎么

Level
A1 #28
Keywords
怎么

怎么 goes right before the verb.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

(Subject +) 怎么 + verb？

Examples

  • 这个字怎么写？zhège zì zěnme xiě?How do you write this character?
  • 这个怎么说？zhège zěnme shuō?How do you say this?

Notes

  • The thing can come first: 芒果怎么吃？ (How do you eat a mango?)
  • 怎么样 is different: "how is it?"

Practice

How do you get to school?你怎么去学校？nǐ zěnme qù xuéxiào?Say it, then tap to check

7. "And you?" with 呢

Level
A1 #15
Keywords
呢

Bounces a question back: 我很好，你呢？

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources Quiz me

Structure

Topic + 呢？

Examples

  • 我在家，你呢？wǒ zài jiā, nǐ ne?I'm at home. What about you?
  • 我的手机呢？wǒ de shǒujī ne?Where's my phone?
  • 爸爸什么时候来北京呢？bàba shénme shíhou lái Běijīng ne?When is Dad coming to Beijing?your notes

Notes

  • A thing + 呢 asks where it is: 我的书呢？ (Where's my book?)
  • 呢 also ends other sentences, softening them or marking an ongoing action.

Practice

I'm good, and you?我很好，你呢？wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?Say it, then tap to check
