Food & drink

Food is one of the easiest ways into a conversation, especially in Taiwan. This page covers drinks, everyday foods, fruit and vegetables, how to describe flavors, and how to order at a restaurant.

Everyday drinks

Drinks use 喝, not 吃.

喝hē喝ㄏㄜto drinkAlso read hè (to shout), as in 吆喝.
水shuǐ水ㄕㄨㄟˇwater
茶chá茶ㄔㄚˊtea
咖啡kāfēi咖啡ㄎㄚ ㄈㄟcoffeeSound-based word.
牛奶niúnǎi牛奶ㄋㄧㄡˊ ㄋㄞˇmilk
果汁guǒzhī果汁ㄍㄨㄛˇ ㄓjuice
可乐kělè可樂ㄎㄜˇ ㄌㄜˋcola
啤酒píjiǔ啤酒ㄆㄧˊ ㄐㄧㄡˇbeerLook-alike: 脾 (spleen). 啤 has the mouth radical 口.
好喝hǎohē好喝ㄏㄠˇ ㄏㄜdelicious (drink)

Examples

  • 你喝茶还是咖啡？nǐ hē chá háishì kāfēi?Tea or coffee?
  • 这个果汁很好喝！zhège guǒzhī hěn hǎohē!This juice is really good!

Cups, bottles and cans

杯子 is the cup itself; 杯 counts what's in it.

杯bēi杯ㄅㄟcup, glassAlone, 杯 is the measure word; the noun is 杯子.
瓶píng瓶ㄆㄧㄥˊbottle
听tīng聽ㄊㄧㄥcan (container)Same character as “to listen.”
罐guàn罐ㄍㄨㄢˋjar, tina jar of honey.
杯子bēizi杯子ㄅㄟ ˙ㄗcup, glass

Examples

  • 我要一杯咖啡。wǒ yào yì bēi kāfēi.A coffee, please.
  • 我要两瓶水。wǒ yào liǎng píng shuǐ.Two bottles of water, please.

Practice

Two bottles of water, please.我要两瓶水。wǒ yào liǎng píng shuǐ.Say it, then tap to check
I don't drink beer.我不喝啤酒。wǒ bù hē píjiǔ.Say it, then tap to check

Staples

Taiwan writes noodles as 麵. 菜 can mean a dish or vegetables.

吃chī吃ㄔto eat
米饭mǐfàn米飯ㄇㄧˇ ㄈㄢˋcooked rice
面miàn麵ㄇㄧㄢˋnoodlesAlso means “face, side.” Taiwan writes noodles as 麵.
面包miànbāo麵包ㄇㄧㄢˋ ㄅㄠbread
鸡蛋jīdàn雞蛋ㄐㄧ ㄉㄢˋegg
菜cài菜ㄘㄞˋdish, course; vegetables

Examples

  • 我要一碗米饭。wǒ yào yì wǎn mǐfàn.A bowl of rice, please.
  • 牛肉面很好吃。niúròu miàn hěn hǎochī.Beef noodles are delicious.

Meat and fish

Animal + 肉 = that meat.

肉ròu肉ㄖㄡˋmeat
牛肉niúròu牛肉ㄋㄧㄡˊ ㄖㄡˋbeef牛肉麵 is a classic Taiwan dish.
猪肉zhūròu豬肉ㄓㄨ ㄖㄡˋpork
鸡肉jīròu雞肉ㄐㄧ ㄖㄡˋchicken (meat)
鱼yú魚ㄩˊfish

Examples

  • 我不吃猪肉。wǒ bù chī zhūròu.I don't eat pork.

Fruit

Pineapple is 凤梨 in Taiwan and 菠萝 on the mainland.

水果shuǐguǒ水果ㄕㄨㄟˇ ㄍㄨㄛˇfruit
苹果píngguǒ蘋果ㄆㄧㄥˊ ㄍㄨㄛˇapple
香蕉xiāngjiāo香蕉ㄒㄧㄤ ㄐㄧㄠbanana
葡萄pútao葡萄ㄆㄨˊ ˙ㄊㄠgrapes
草莓cǎoméi草莓ㄘㄠˇ ㄇㄟˊstrawberry
橘子júzi橘子ㄐㄩˊ ˙ㄗtangerine
西瓜xīguā西瓜ㄒㄧ ㄍㄨㄚwatermelon
芒果mángguǒ芒果ㄇㄤˊ ㄍㄨㄛˇmango
凤梨fènglí鳳梨ㄈㄥˋ ㄌㄧˊpineapple (Taiwan word)Mainland says 菠萝.
菠萝bōluó菠蘿ㄅㄛ ㄌㄨㄛˊpineappleTaiwan usually says 鳳梨 (fènglí).
柠檬níngméng檸檬ㄋㄧㄥˊ ㄇㄥˊlemon

Examples

  • 我最喜欢芒果。wǒ zuì xǐhuan mángguǒ.Mango is my favorite.
  • 台湾的凤梨很甜。Táiwān de fènglí hěn tián.Taiwan's pineapples are sweet.

Vegetables

Careful: 土豆 is a potato on the mainland but a peanut in Taiwan.

蔬菜shūcài蔬菜ㄕㄨ ㄘㄞˋvegetables
青菜qīngcài青菜ㄑㄧㄥ ㄘㄞˋleafy greensCommon at Taiwan restaurants: 燙青菜.
番茄fānqié番茄ㄈㄢ ㄑㄧㄝˊtomatoAlso 西红柿 on the mainland.
洋葱yángcōng洋蔥ㄧㄤˊ ㄘㄨㄥonion
土豆tǔdòu土豆ㄊㄨˇ ㄉㄡˋpotato (mainland)In Taiwan 土豆 is a peanut; potato is 馬鈴薯.

Examples

  • 我要一盘青菜。wǒ yào yì pán qīngcài.A plate of greens, please.

Snacks

饼干bǐnggān餅乾ㄅㄧㄥˇ ㄍㄢcookie, biscuit

Examples

  • 我想吃饼干。wǒ xiǎng chī bǐnggān.I want some cookies.

Practice

I don't eat beef.我不吃牛肉。wǒ bù chī niúròu.Say it, then tap to check
I like bananas.我喜欢香蕉。wǒ xǐhuan xiāngjiāo.Say it, then tap to check

3. Describing flavors

Used for
Food & flavorsDescribing thingsReacting & agreeing
Keywords
咸、甜、苦、辣、酸、好吃、好喝

Flavors work like other adjectives: 很 + flavor, or 太 + flavor + 了 for "too."

The five flavors Tasty or not Practice Sources Quiz me

The five flavors

酸suān酸ㄙㄨㄢsour
甜tián甜ㄊㄧㄢˊsweet
苦kǔ苦ㄎㄨˇbitter
辣là辣ㄌㄚˋspicy
咸xián鹹ㄒㄧㄢˊsalty

Examples

  • 这个菜很辣！zhège cài hěn là!This dish is really spicy!
  • 太咸了！tài xián le!Too salty!

Tasty or not

Add 不 for “not good”: 不好吃.

好吃hǎochī好吃ㄏㄠˇ ㄔdelicious (food)
好喝hǎohē好喝ㄏㄠˇ ㄏㄜdelicious (drink)

Examples

  • 不好吃。bù hǎochī.It's not good.

Practice

Too spicy!太辣了！tài là le!Say it, then tap to check
This is delicious!这个很好吃！zhège hěn hǎochī!Say it, then tap to check

Places to eat

In Taiwan, 饭店 usually means a hotel.

饭馆fànguǎn飯館ㄈㄢˋ ㄍㄨㄢˇrestaurant
饭店fàndiàn飯店ㄈㄢˋ ㄉㄧㄢˋrestaurant, hotelIn Taiwan, usually a hotel.
服务员fúwùyuán服務員ㄈㄨˊ ㄨˋ ㄩㄢˊwaiter, server

Examples

  • 我们去饭馆吃饭吧。wǒmen qù fànguǎn chīfàn ba.Let's go eat at a restaurant.

Ordering

The measure word shows how the food comes.

你要吃什么？nǐ yào chī shénme?你要吃什麼？ㄋㄧˇ ㄧㄠˋ ㄔ ㄕㄣˊ ˙ㄇㄜ？what do you want to eat?
碗wǎn碗ㄨㄢˇfood in a bowla bowl of noodles.
盘pán盤ㄆㄢˊa plate or dish of food
份fèn份ㄈㄣˋa set meal, an orderan order of rice.
串chuàn串ㄔㄨㄢˋskewer
笼lóng籠ㄌㄨㄥˊsteamer basket
锅guō鍋ㄍㄨㄛpot, pana pot of soup.
碟dié碟ㄉㄧㄝˊsmall plate or dish
片piàn片ㄆㄧㄢˋthin slicea slice of bread.
顿dùn頓ㄉㄨㄣˋa meal

Examples

  • 我们要一笼小笼包。wǒmen yào yì lóng xiǎolóngbāo.A basket of soup dumplings, please.
  • 你要吃什么？nǐ yào chī shénme?What do you want to eat?

Paying

我请你wǒ qǐng nǐ我請你ㄨㄛˇ ㄑㄧㄥˇ ㄋㄧˇmy treat
请qǐng請ㄑㄧㄥˇplease, to invite
吃饭chīfàn吃飯ㄔ ㄈㄢˋto eat a meal

Examples

  • 今天我请你！jīntiān wǒ qǐng nǐ!Today's my treat!

Practice

A bowl of rice, please.我要一碗米饭。wǒ yào yì wǎn mǐfàn.Say it, then tap to check
My treat!我请你！wǒ qǐng nǐ!Say it, then tap to check
