1. Daily routine

Used for
Daily routine
Keywords
做、看、吃饭、听到、洗澡、看到、看见、睡觉

Daily routines are a time word + a verb: 我七点起床 (I get up at seven).

Morning and night Seeing and hearing Doing things Practice Sources

Morning and night

起床 get up · 洗澡 shower · 吃饭 eat · 睡觉 go to sleep.

起床qǐchuáng起床ㄑㄧˇ ㄔㄨㄤˊget out of bed
洗澡xǐzǎo洗澡ㄒㄧˇ ㄗㄠˇto take a shower
吃饭chīfàn吃飯ㄔ ㄈㄢˋto eat a meal
睡觉shuìjiào睡覺ㄕㄨㄟˋ ㄐㄧㄠˋto sleep, go to bed

Examples

  • 我晚上洗澡。wǒ wǎnshang xǐzǎo.I shower in the evening.
  • 你几点睡觉？nǐ jǐ diǎn shuìjiào?What time do you go to bed?

Seeing and hearing

看 is look, watch or read. 看到 and 看见 mean you actually saw it; 听到 means you heard it.

看kàn看ㄎㄢˋto look, read
看到kàndào看到ㄎㄢˋ ㄉㄠˋto see
看见kànjiàn看見ㄎㄢˋ ㄐㄧㄢˋto seeLike 看到: you actually saw it.
听到tīngdào聽到ㄊㄧㄥ ㄉㄠˋto hear

Examples

  • 我看到你了！wǒ kàndào nǐ le!I see you!

Doing things

做 is "do" or "make": 做饭 (cook), 做什么 (do what).

做zuò做ㄗㄨㄛˋto do, to makeSame sound as 坐 (to sit).

Examples

  • 你在做什么？nǐ zài zuò shénme?What are you doing?

Practice

I get up at eight.我八点起床。wǒ bā diǎn qǐchuáng.Say it, then tap to check
I didn't hear it.我没听到。wǒ méi tīngdào.Say it, then tap to check

2. Around the house

Used for
Around the houseSchool & workShopping & money
Keywords
书、住、家、床、把、灯、门、东西

Say what's in a room with place + 有 + thing: 客厅里有沙发 (there's a sofa in the living room).

Rooms Furniture Things at home Practice Sources

Rooms

Most rooms end in 室 (room) or 房 / 厅 (hall): 卧室, 厨房, 客厅.

家jiā家ㄐㄧㄚhome, family
房间fángjiān房間ㄈㄤˊ ㄐㄧㄢroom
客厅kètīng客廳ㄎㄜˋ ㄊㄧㄥliving room
卧室wòshì臥室ㄨㄛˋ ㄕˋbedroom
厨房chúfáng廚房ㄔㄨˊ ㄈㄤˊkitchen
浴室yùshì浴室ㄩˋ ㄕˋbathroom
住zhù住ㄓㄨˋto live; to stop

Examples

  • 我家有三个房间。wǒ jiā yǒu sān ge fángjiān.My home has three rooms.
  • 你住在哪里？nǐ zhù zài nǎlǐ?Where do you live?

Furniture

床 bed · 沙发 sofa · 桌子 table · 椅子 chair. 把 counts chairs and things with a handle.

床chuáng床ㄔㄨㄤˊbed起床 = get out of bed.
沙发shāfā沙發ㄕㄚ ㄈㄚsofaSound-based word.
桌子zhuōzi桌子ㄓㄨㄛ ˙ㄗtable, desk
椅子yǐzi椅子ㄧˇ ˙ㄗchair
把bǎ把ㄅㄚˇthings held in one hand
门mén門ㄇㄣˊdoor
窗户chuānghu窗戶ㄔㄨㄤ ˙ㄏㄨwindow
灯dēng燈ㄉㄥlight, lamp

Examples

  • 客厅里有一个沙发。kètīng lǐ yǒu yí ge shāfā.There's a sofa in the living room.
  • 有两把椅子。yǒu liǎng bǎ yǐzi.There are two chairs.

Things at home

电视, 电脑, 冰箱: many machines start with 电 (electric). 东西 means "things, stuff."

电视diànshì電視ㄉㄧㄢˋ ㄕˋTV
电脑diànnǎo電腦ㄉㄧㄢˋ ㄋㄠˇcomputer
冰箱bīngxiāng冰箱ㄅㄧㄥ ㄒㄧㄤfridge
书shū書ㄕㄨbook
衣服yīfu衣服ㄧ ˙ㄈㄨclothes
东西dōngxi東西ㄉㄨㄥ ˙ㄒㄧthing, stuff
杯子bēizi杯子ㄅㄟ ˙ㄗcup, glass

Examples

  • 桌子上有一个杯子。zhuōzi shàng yǒu yí ge bēizi.There's a cup on the table.
  • 我在家看电视。wǒ zài jiā kàn diànshì.I'm watching TV at home.

Practice

There's a bed in the bedroom.卧室里有一张床。wòshì lǐ yǒu yì zhāng chuáng.Say it, then tap to check

Studying People at school and work Practice Sources

Studying

学 + subject: 学中文. To read a book, say 看书, not 学书.

学校xuéxiào學校ㄒㄩㄝˊ ㄒㄧㄠˋschool
学xué學ㄒㄩㄝˊto learn, study
学习xuéxí學習ㄒㄩㄝˊ ㄒㄧˊto study学习 is the fuller verb; 学 alone is common in speech.
读dú讀ㄉㄨˊto read (aloud)Reading aloud or studying a text. 看书 is reading silently.
写xiě寫ㄒㄧㄝˇto write
字zì字ㄗˋcharacter, wordLook-alike: 子 (as in 儿子).
汉字Hànzì漢字ㄏㄢˋ ㄗˋChinese character
本běn本ㄅㄣˇbooks这本书.

Examples

  • 我在学中文。wǒ zài xué Zhōngwén.I'm learning Chinese.
  • 这个字怎么写？zhège zì zěnme xiě?How do you write this character?

People at school and work

同学tóngxué同學ㄊㄨㄥˊ ㄒㄩㄝˊclassmate
公司gōngsī公司ㄍㄨㄥ ㄙcompany
工作gōngzuò工作ㄍㄨㄥ ㄗㄨㄛˋto work; job

Examples

4. Animals and plants

Pets

狗 dog, 猫 cat. 只 is the usual measure word for animals: 一只猫.

狗gǒu狗ㄍㄡˇdog
猫māo貓ㄇㄠcat
只zhī只ㄓmost animals and birds

Examples

  • 我有一只狗。wǒ yǒu yì zhī gǒu.I have a dog.

Farm animals

These also name meats: 牛 → 牛肉 (beef), 猪 → 猪肉 (pork), 鸡 → 鸡肉 (chicken).

牛niú牛ㄋㄧㄡˊcow
猪zhū豬ㄓㄨpig
鸡jī雞ㄐㄧchicken
马mǎ馬ㄇㄚˇhorse
鸟niǎo鳥ㄋㄧㄠˇbird
兔子tùzi兔子ㄊㄨˋ ˙ㄗrabbit
鱼yú魚ㄩˊfish

Examples

  • 我喜欢兔子。wǒ xǐhuan tùzi.I like rabbits.

Measure words for animals and plants

条 long things (fish, snakes) · 头 large animals · 匹 horses · 朵 flowers · 棵 trees · 株 plants · 根 thin things · 颗 small round things · 粒 grains · 瓣 cloves.

条tiáo條ㄊㄧㄠˊlong, thin animalsfish, snakes, dragons.
头tóu頭ㄊㄡˊlarge animals; bulb vegetablespigs, onions.
匹pǐ匹ㄆㄧˇhorses, mules
朵duǒ朵ㄉㄨㄛˇflowers; clouds
棵kē棵ㄎㄜtrees, grass, cabbage
株zhū株ㄓㄨsmaller trees and plantsflowers still in the ground.
根gēn根ㄍㄣlong, thin objectsa carrot, thin strands.
颗kē顆ㄎㄜsmall round objectsa cherry.
粒lì粒ㄌㄧˋgrains, small round thingsrice.
瓣bàn瓣ㄅㄢˋsegments, clovesa clove of garlic.

Examples

  • 一条鱼yì tiáo yúa fish
  • 一朵花yì duǒ huāa flower
