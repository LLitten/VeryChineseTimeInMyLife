Daily routine

Words

起床qǐchuáng起床ㄑㄧˇ ㄔㄨㄤˊget out of bed
洗澡xǐzǎo洗澡ㄒㄧˇ ㄗㄠˇto take a shower
吃饭chīfàn吃飯ㄔ ㄈㄢˋto eat a meal
睡觉shuìjiào睡覺ㄕㄨㄟˋ ㄐㄧㄠˋto sleep, go to bed
看kàn看ㄎㄢˋto look, read
看到kàndào看到ㄎㄢˋ ㄉㄠˋto see
看见kànjiàn看見ㄎㄢˋ ㄐㄧㄢˋto seeLike 看到: you actually saw it.
听到tīngdào聽到ㄊㄧㄥ ㄉㄠˋto hear
做zuò做ㄗㄨㄛˋto do, to makeSame sound as 坐 (to sit).

How to use them

  • Time goes before the verb: 我十一点睡觉.
  • 看 = look, watch or read. 看到 and 看见 = actually saw it.
  • 做 = do or make: 做饭 (cook).

Examples

我晚上洗澡。wǒ wǎnshang xǐzǎo.I shower in the evening.
你几点睡觉？nǐ jǐ diǎn shuìjiào?What time do you go to bed?
我看到你了！wǒ kàndào nǐ le!I see you!

Try it

I didn't hear it.我没听到。wǒ méi tīngdào.Say it, then tap to check

Around the house

Words

家jiā家ㄐㄧㄚhome, family
桌子zhuōzi桌子ㄓㄨㄛ ˙ㄗtable, desk
椅子yǐzi椅子ㄧˇ ˙ㄗchair
电视diànshì電視ㄉㄧㄢˋ ㄕˋTV
电脑diànnǎo電腦ㄉㄧㄢˋ ㄋㄠˇcomputer
书shū書ㄕㄨbook
衣服yīfu衣服ㄧ ˙ㄈㄨclothes
东西dōngxi東西ㄉㄨㄥ ˙ㄒㄧthing, stuff
杯子bēizi杯子ㄅㄟ ˙ㄗcup, glass
把bǎ把ㄅㄚˇthings held in one hand

How to use them

  • Place + 有 + thing = there is: 桌子上有书.
  • 把 counts things you hold, or with a handle: 一把椅子.
  • 杯子 is the cup itself. 一杯水 is a glass of water.

Examples

桌子上有一个杯子。zhuōzi shàng yǒu yí ge bēizi.There's a cup on the table.
我在家看电视。wǒ zài jiā kàn diànshì.I'm watching TV at home.
你住在哪里？nǐ zhù zài nǎlǐ?Where do you live?

Try it

There are two chairs.有两把椅子。yǒu liǎng bǎ yǐzi.Say it, then tap to check

学xué學ㄒㄩㄝˊto learn, study
学习xuéxí學習ㄒㄩㄝˊ ㄒㄧˊto study学习 is the fuller verb; 学 alone is common in speech.
读dú讀ㄉㄨˊto read (aloud)Reading aloud or studying a text. 看书 is reading silently.
写xiě寫ㄒㄧㄝˇto write
字zì字ㄗˋcharacter, wordLook-alike: 子 (as in 儿子).
汉字Hànzì漢字ㄏㄢˋ ㄗˋChinese character
本běn本ㄅㄣˇbooks这本书.

How to use them

  • 学 + subject: 学中文. To read a book, say 看书, not 学书.
  • 本 counts books: 一本书.
  • 在 + place + 工作 = work at: 在公司工作.

Examples

我在学中文。wǒ zài xué Zhōngwén.I'm learning Chinese.
这个字怎么写？zhège zì zěnme xiě?How do you write this character?
我有三本书。wǒ yǒu sān běn shū.I have three books.

Try it

I work at a company.我在公司工作。wǒ zài gōngsī gōngzuò.Say it, then tap to check

How to use them

  • 只 is the usual measure word for animals: 一只猫.
  • 条 is for long, thin things: 一条鱼 (a fish).
  • 朵 flowers · 棵 trees · 颗 small round things · 粒 grains.

Examples
