Jobs & free time

"What do you do?" and "What do you like to do?" are two of the most common questions when getting to know someone. This page covers jobs, hobbies and sports so you can answer both.

Examples

爱好 is "hobby." Ask 你的爱好是什么？ or 你喜欢做什么？ and answer with 我喜欢 + activity.

Playing ball Other sports Practice Sources

Playing ball

Ball games played with the hands use 打 (hit): 打篮球. Football uses 踢 (kick): 踢足球.

运动yùndòng運動ㄩㄣˋ ㄉㄨㄥˋsports, exercise
打篮球dǎ lánqiú打籃球ㄉㄚˇ ㄌㄢˊ ㄑㄧㄡˊto play basketballBall games with hands use 打.
踢足球tī zúqiú踢足球ㄊㄧ ㄗㄨˊ ㄑㄧㄡˊto play soccer

Examples

  • 我们去打篮球吧！wǒmen qù dǎ lánqiú ba!Let's go play basketball!
