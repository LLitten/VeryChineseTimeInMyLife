1. 不 + verb / adjective

Level
A1 #2
Keywords
不

For now, the future, habits, and things you don't want to do.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

Subject + 不 + verb / adjective

Examples

  • 我不吃鱼。wǒ bù chī yú.I don't eat fish.
  • 他不高。tā bù gāo.He isn't tall.
  • 我们不喝酒。wǒmen bù hē jiǔ.We don't drink alcohol.AllSet

Notes

  • 不 is bù, but bú before a 4th tone: 不是 bú shì.
  • Never 不有. 有 only takes 没.

Practice

I'm not going.我不去。wǒ bú qù.Say it, then tap to check

2. 没(有) + verb: didn't

Level
A1 #29
Keywords
有、没

For things that didn't happen, or haven't happened yet.

Structure Examples Notes Watch out Practice Sources

Structure

Subject + 没(有) + verb

Examples

  • 我昨天没吃饭。wǒ zuótiān méi chīfàn.I didn't eat yesterday.
  • 我没有去上班。wǒ méiyǒu qù shàngbān.I didn't go to work.AllSet

Notes

  • 没钱了 ("out of money") is a different 了, and it's fine.

Watch out

  • No 了 after the verb: ✗ 我没吃了.

Practice

I didn't go yesterday.我昨天没去。wǒ zuótiān méi qù.Say it, then tap to check

3. 没有 + noun: don't have

Level
A1 #1
Keywords
有、没

The negative of 有 (have, there is).

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

没(有) + noun

Examples

  • 我没有书。wǒ méiyǒu shū.I don't have a book.
  • 没有办法。méiyǒu bànfǎ.There's no way.your notes

Notes

  • 没有 can shorten to 没.
  • The number and measure word usually drop out: 我没有书.

Practice

I don't have money.我没有钱。wǒ méiyǒu qián.Say it, then tap to check

4. Don't: 别 / 不要

Level
A1 #30
Keywords
别、不要

Usually no subject, as in English "Don't …".

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

别 / 不要 + verb

Examples

  • 别走！bié zǒu!Don't go!
  • 不要说英文。búyào shuō Yīngwén.Don't speak English.AllSet
  • 别说了。bié shuō le.Stop talking.
  • 别气我。bié qì wǒ.Don't make me mad.your notes

Notes

  • 不要 is said búyào. 别 and 不要 are mostly interchangeable.
  • Add 了 to mean "stop": 别说了. It can also mean "don't (yet)": 别买了，太贵了.

Practice

Stop crying!别哭了！bié kū le!Say it, then tap to check

Sources

  • AllSet: Negative commands with "buyao"
  • Yoyo Chinese: Grammar Series, Lesson 6 (别 and 不要)
  • AllSet: Expressing "stop doing" with "bie… le"
  • Wording and unmarked examples: Claude

5. 不太 + adjective: not very

Level
A2
Keywords
不太

The opposite of 太: "not too," "not very."

Structure Examples Notes Watch out Practice Sources

Structure

Subject + 不太 + adjective

Examples

  • 我不太高兴。wǒ bú tài gāoxìng.I'm not very happy.
  • 我家不太大。wǒ jiā bú tài dà.My home isn't very big.AllSet

Notes

  • Works with a few feeling verbs too: 不太喜欢.
  • Said bú tài.

Watch out

  • No 了: 不太好, not ✗ 不太好了.

Practice

I don't really like it.我不太喜欢。wǒ bú tài xǐhuan.Say it, then tap to check

Sources

  • AllSet: Expressing "not very" with "bu tai"
  • Wording and unmarked examples: Claude

6. Not as … as: 没有

Level
A2
Keywords
没有

A is not as … as B. A has less of it.

Structure Examples Practice Sources

Structure

A + 没有 + B + adjective

Examples

  • 你没有我高。nǐ méiyǒu wǒ gāo.You're not as tall as me.your notes
  • 今天没有昨天热。jīntiān méiyǒu zuótiān rè.Today isn't as hot as yesterday.

Practice

Tea isn't as good as coffee.茶没有咖啡好喝。chá méiyǒu kāfēi hǎohē.Say it, then tap to check
