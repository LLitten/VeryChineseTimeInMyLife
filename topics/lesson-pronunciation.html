1. Tones

Every syllable has a tone, and the tone changes the meaning: mā (mom) and mǎ (horse) are different words.

The four tones Tones in zhuyin Sources Quiz me

The four tones

1st high and flat: mā · 2nd rising: má · 3rd low, dipping: mǎ · 4th falling: mà · neutral, light: ma.

Examples

  • 妈 / 麻 / 马 / 骂mā / má / mǎ / màmom / hemp / horse / scold

Tones in zhuyin

In zhuyin, the 1st tone has no mark. The others are ˊ ˇ ˋ, and the neutral tone is a dot in front: ˙ㄇㄚ.

2. Tone changes

A few common words change tone depending on the next syllable.

不 一 Two 3rd tones Sources Quiz me

不

不 is bù, but becomes bú before a 4th tone: 不是 bú shì.

Examples

  • 不客气bú kèqiyou're welcome

一

一 is yī alone, yí before a 4th tone (一个 yí ge), and yì before the others (一起 yìqǐ).

Examples

  • 一点儿yìdiǎnra little

Two 3rd tones

When two 3rd tones meet, the first is said as a 2nd tone. 你好 sounds like ní hǎo.

3. Zhuyin

Keywords
ㄅ、ㄆ、ㄇ、ㄈ、ㄉ、ㄊ、ㄋ、ㄌ

Zhuyin (also called bopomofo) is how Taiwan writes pronunciation. Each symbol is one sound, and a syllable is initial + final + tone: ㄏㄠˇ = hǎo.

4. The vowel e

Pinyin e is a common trouble spot.

Two sounds Sources Quiz me

Two sounds

On its own, e sounds roughly like "uh": 喝 hē, 的 de. In -ie and -ei it's closer to "eh": 谢谢 xièxie, 北 běi.
