Tones

How to use them

  • Mandarin has four tones plus a light neutral tone.
  • 1st high and flat: mā · 2nd rising: má · 3rd low, dipping: mǎ · 4th falling: mà · neutral: ma.
  • In zhuyin, the 1st tone has no mark. The others are ˊ ˇ ˋ, and the neutral tone is a dot in front: ˙ㄇㄚ.

Examples

妈 / 麻 / 马 / 骂mā / má / mǎ / màmom / hemp / horse / scold

Tone changes

How to use them

  • 不 becomes bú before a 4th tone: 不是 bú shì.
  • 一 is yī alone, yí before a 4th tone (一个 yí ge), and yì before the others (一起 yìqǐ).
  • Two 3rd tones in a row: the first is said as a 2nd tone. 你好 sounds like ní hǎo.

Examples

不客气bú kèqiyou're welcome
一点儿yìdiǎnra little

How to use them

  • Zhuyin (also called bopomofo) is how Taiwan writes pronunciation.
  • Each symbol is one sound. Initials start a syllable.
  • ㄐ ㄑ ㄒ (j q x) and ㄓ ㄔ ㄕ ㄖ (zh ch sh r) have no close English sound.

How to use them

  • Finals are the vowel part of a syllable.
  • A syllable is initial + final + tone: ㄏㄠˇ = hǎo.

The vowel e

How to use them

  • Pinyin e sounds roughly like "uh": 喝 hē, 的 de.
  • The "eh" sound is e in -ie and -ei: 谢谢 xièxie, 北 běi.
