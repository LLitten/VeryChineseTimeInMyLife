Key words

买mǎi買ㄇㄞˇto buy
买到mǎidào買到ㄇㄞˇ ㄉㄠˋto manage to buy
钱qián錢ㄑㄧㄢˊmoney
块kuài塊ㄎㄨㄞˋpiece, chunk; yuana piece of cake. Spoken word for yuan (money).
多少duōshao多少ㄉㄨㄛ ˙ㄕㄠhow much, how many
东西dōngxi東西ㄉㄨㄥ ˙ㄒㄧthing, stuff
衣服yīfu衣服ㄧ ˙ㄈㄨclothes
包bāo包ㄅㄠpack, baga bag of chips.
盒hé盒ㄏㄜˊbox, carton

Examples

  • 这个多少钱？ 一百块。zhège duōshao qián? yì bǎi kuài.How much is this? 100 dollars.
  • 我想买衣服。wǒ xiǎng mǎi yīfu.I want to buy some clothes.
  • 我要两盒。wǒ yào liǎng hé.I'll take two boxes.

Notes

  • 块 is the spoken word for a dollar or yuan: 五十块.
  • 买到 = managed to buy.
