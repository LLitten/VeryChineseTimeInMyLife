People

Meeting people is where most conversations start. This page covers greeting friends, saying goodbye, introducing yourself, and talking about the people in your life. Most of it is short, fixed phrases you can use right away.

5. Example dialogue

Two friends run into each other. B brings a friend, C, who is from Taipei.

Dialogue Sources Quiz me

Dialogue

  • A嗨！好久不见！hāi! hǎojiǔ bújiàn!Hi! Long time no see!
  • B好久不见！最近怎么样？hǎojiǔ bújiàn! zuìjìn zěnmeyàng?Long time no see! How have you been?
  • A还不错。你呢？hái búcuò. nǐ ne?Pretty good. And you?
  • B我很好。这是我朋友，她叫美美。wǒ hěn hǎo. zhè shì wǒ péngyou, tā jiào Měiměi.I'm good. This is my friend Meimei.
  • A你好！你是哪里人？nǐ hǎo! nǐ shì nǎlǐ rén?Hi! Where are you from?
  • C我是台北人。你呢？wǒ shì Táiběi rén. nǐ ne?I'm from Taipei. And you?
  • A我是美国人。我在学中文。wǒ shì Měiguó rén. wǒ zài xué Zhōngwén.I'm American. I'm learning Chinese.
  • C你说得很好！nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo!You speak really well!
  • A没有啦！méiyǒu la!Oh, not really!
