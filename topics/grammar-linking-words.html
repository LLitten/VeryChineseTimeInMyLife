Linking words

和, 还是 and 或者, and the two jobs of 的.

  • 和: and, with
  • Or: 还是 and 或者
  • 的: possession
  • Close people: no 的
  • 的: description + noun

和: and, with A1 #5

Noun + 和 + noun

How it works

  • Joins nouns only, not verbs or sentences.
  • It isn't every English "and."

Examples

我和朋友wǒ hé péngyoumy friend and I
咖啡和茶都很好喝。kāfēi hé chá dōu hěn hǎohē.Coffee and tea are both good.

Try it

Or: 还是 and 或者 A1 #6

A + 还是 + B？ (questions) · A + 或者 + B (statements)

How it works

  • 还是 is "or" in a question, when the listener should pick one.
  • 或者 is "or" in a statement. It's an A2 point.
  • Statements often end with 都可以 (either is fine).

Examples

你想喝咖啡还是茶？nǐ xiǎng hē kāfēi háishì chá?Do you want coffee or tea?
我喝咖啡或者茶，都可以。wǒ hē kāfēi huòzhě chá, dōu kěyǐ.Coffee or tea, either is fine.

Try it

Today or tomorrow?今天还是明天？jīntiān háishì míngtiān?Say it, then tap to check

的: possession A1 #14

Noun 1 + 的 + noun 2

How it works

  • "Noun 1's noun 2": 我的书 (my book).

Examples

我的书wǒ de shūmy book
老师的电脑lǎoshī de diànnǎothe teacher's computer

Close people: no 的 A1 #13

Pronoun + noun

How it works

  • Drop 的 for family, close friends, partners, and your school or workplace.
  • Keeping 的 there sounds distant.

Examples

我妈妈wǒ māmamy mom
我朋友wǒ péngyoumy friend
我们学校wǒmen xuéxiàoour school

Try it

my dad我爸爸wǒ bàbaSay it, then tap to check

的: description + noun A2

Description + 的 + noun

How it works

  • Links a description to a noun: 好吃的菜 (tasty food).
  • Drop the noun to mean "the … one": 大的 (the big one).

Examples

好吃的菜hǎochī de càitasty food
我要大的。wǒ yào dà de.I want the big one.
