Verbs and helping verbs

Grammar section 6 of 10. Tags show the AllSet level.

喜欢 + verb Beyond A1

我喜欢吃饭。 I like eating.

想 + verb Beyond A1

want to, would like to (softer than 要).

我想买水。 I'd like to buy water.

要 + verb: want A1 #27

我要喝水。 I want water.

要 + verb: going to A1 #25

Note: Don't use 在 for the future: 在 + verb means happening right now.

我明天要去北京。 I'm going to Beijing tomorrow. 他就要来了。 He's about to arrive.

会 + verb A1 #24

Note: 会 also predicts the future: 明天会很好 (tomorrow will be good).

我会说中文。 I can speak Chinese. 我会画画。 I can draw. (your notes)

能 + verb A1 #23

able to, when circumstances allow.

我能吃一块吗？ Can I have a piece?

  • Note: 可能 = maybe.

可以 + verb A1 #26

may, be allowed to.

我可以坐这儿吗？ May I sit here? 可以。 Sure.

得 + verb / 不用 + verb Beyond A1

have to / don't need to.

我得走了。 I have to go. 你不用来。 You don't need to come.

  • Note: Not 不得.

让 + person + verb Beyond A1

let, make or ask someone to do something.

他让我睡觉。 He lets me sleep. 你让我很高兴。 You make me happy.

想让 + person + verb Beyond A1

want someone to do something.

我想让你学习。 I want you to study.

去 + place A1 #21

Note: No 在 needed; 去 already carries the place.

我明天去学校。 I'm going to school tomorrow.

叫 + name A1 #20

我叫Sam。 My name is Sam. 你叫什么名字？ What's your name?

姓 + surname A1 #22

我姓王。 My surname is Wang. 您贵姓？ What's your surname? (polite)
