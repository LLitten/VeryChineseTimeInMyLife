Numbers, measure words, dates and time

Numbers in Chinese are very regular, but using them needs a few rules: 二 vs 两, measure words, and the order of dates and times. This page covers them all.

1. Building numbers

Level
A1 #11
Keywords
二十二、一百零五

11–19: 十 + number. Tens: number + 十.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

Big to small: 二十二, 一百零五

Examples

  • 二十二èrshí'èr22
  • 三百零五sānbǎi líng wǔ305AllSet

Notes

  • A zero in the middle is said once as 零: 一百零一 (101).
  • In phone numbers, 1 is often yāo.

Practice

2. 二 vs 两

Level
A1 #40
Keywords
两、二

二 is the plain number: counting, 十二, 第二 (2nd), 二号 (the 2nd).

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

二 for numbers · 两 + measure word

Examples

  • 两个小时liǎng ge xiǎoshítwo hoursAllSet
  • 十二shí'èr12

Notes

  • 两 goes before a measure word: 两个人, 两本书.
  • 2 o'clock is 两点.

Practice

two people两个人liǎng ge rénSay it, then tap to check

3. Measure words

Level
A1 #8

Every counted noun needs a measure word.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

Number + measure word + noun

Examples

  • 一个人yí ge rénone person
  • 四杯牛奶sì bēi niúnǎifour glasses of milkAllSet
  • 三本书sān běn shūthree books

Notes

  • 个 is the most common, and works when you're unsure.
  • With "one," 一 can drop: 我要个苹果.
  • Common ones: 本 books · 杯 cups · 只 animals · 条 long things.

Practice

two cups of tea两杯茶liǎng bēi cháSay it, then tap to check

4. Age with 岁

Level
A1 #7
Keywords
岁

No 是 and no 个: 我二十五岁.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

Subject + number + 岁

Examples

  • 他女儿七岁。tā nǚ'ér qī suì.His daughter is seven.your notes
  • 我儿子一岁半。wǒ érzi yí suì bàn.My son is one and a half.

Notes

  • 岁半 = and a half.

Practice

I'm thirty.我三十岁。wǒ sānshí suì.Say it, then tap to check

5. Dates

Level
A1 #9
Keywords
号、年、日、月

Big to small, like addresses.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

Year + 年 + month + 月 + day + 号 / 日

Examples

  • 今天几月几号？jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào?What's the date today?
  • 一月一日是新年。yī yuè yī rì shì Xīnnián.January 1st is New Year's Day.AllSet

Notes

  • 号 is spoken. 日 is written.

Practice

My birthday is May 3rd.我的生日是五月三号。wǒ de shēngrì shì wǔ yuè sān hào.Say it, then tap to check

6. Days of the week

Level
A1 #10
Keywords
星期

星期一 Monday … 星期六 Saturday. The week starts on Monday.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

星期 + number

Examples

  • 今天星期几？jīntiān xīngqī jǐ?What day is it today?
  • 今天是星期一吗？jīntiān shì xīngqīyī ma?Is today Monday?AllSet

Notes

  • Sunday is 星期天 or 星期日.
  • 是 is optional: 今天星期五.

Practice

Tomorrow is Sunday.明天星期天。míngtiān xīngqītiān.Say it, then tap to check

7. Clock time

Level
A1 #12
Keywords
分、点、点半

两点 for 2 o'clock, not 二点.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

Number + 点 (+ number + 分) · number + 点半

Examples

  • 现在十点。xiànzài shí diǎn.It's 10 o'clock.
  • 下午两点半xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn bàn2:30 p.m.AllSet

Notes

  • 半 = half past: 两点半.
  • 上午 a.m., 下午 p.m.: 下午三点.

Practice

It's 3:20.现在三点二十分。xiànzài sān diǎn èrshí fēn.Say it, then tap to check
