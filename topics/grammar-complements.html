Complements (what happens after the verb)

Verb + 到 for results, and verb + 来 / 去 for direction.

Also covered in Yoyo Chinese: Grammar Series, Unit 8: Complements of Result · Unit 9: Complements of Direction

  • Verb + 到: you got the result
  • Verb + 来 / 去: which direction

Verb + 到: you got the result A2

Verb + 到 (+ object)

How it works

  • Shows the action succeeded: 找 = look for, 找到 = found.
  • 看到, 听到 = actually saw, heard. 看见 and 听见 mean the same.
  • Negate with 没: 我没看到. "Can't" is verb + 不到: 找不到.

Examples

我找到了！wǒ zhǎodào le!I found it!
我找不到我的书。wǒ zhǎo bu dào wǒ de shū.I can't find my book.
你看到他了吗？nǐ kàndào tā le ma?Did you see him?

Try it

I didn't hear it.我没听到。wǒ méi tīngdào.Say it, then tap to check

Verb + 来 / 去: which direction B1

Verb + 来 (toward you) / 去 (away from you)

How it works

  • 来 = toward the speaker. 去 = away from the speaker.
  • 上来 come up · 上去 go up · 下来 come down · 下去 go down.
  • A place goes before 来 / 去: 回中国来, not ❌ 回来中国.

Examples

你下来吧！nǐ xiàlái ba!Come down!
我在楼上，你上来。wǒ zài lóushàng, nǐ shànglái.I'm upstairs. Come up.AllSet

Try it

Come down!下来！xiàlái!Say it, then tap to check
