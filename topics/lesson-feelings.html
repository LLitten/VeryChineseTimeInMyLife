Feelings work like other describing words: 我很开心 (I'm happy). Add 不太 for "not very," or 太…了 for "so."

Good feelings

开心 is casual; 高兴 is a bit more formal.

开心kāixīn開心ㄎㄞ ㄒㄧㄣhappy, having funMore casual than 高兴.
高兴gāoxìng高興ㄍㄠ ㄒㄧㄥˋhappy
兴奋xīngfèn興奮ㄒㄧㄥ ㄈㄣˋexcited

Examples

  • 今天我很开心！jīntiān wǒ hěn kāixīn!I'm really happy today!
  • 我好兴奋！wǒ hǎo xīngfèn!I'm so excited!

Bad feelings

难过nánguò難過ㄋㄢˊ ㄍㄨㄛˋsad, upset
生气shēngqì生氣ㄕㄥ ㄑㄧˋangry
害怕hàipà害怕ㄏㄞˋ ㄆㄚˋscared
失望shīwàng失望ㄕ ㄨㄤˋdisappointed

Examples

  • 你生气了吗？nǐ shēngqì le ma?Are you angry?
  • 我不太难过。wǒ bú tài nánguò.I'm not too sad.

Nervous and worried

紧张jǐnzhāng緊張ㄐㄧㄣˇ ㄓㄤnervous
担心dānxīn擔心ㄉㄢ ㄒㄧㄣto worry
别担心bié dānxīn別擔心ㄅㄧㄝˊ ㄉㄢ ㄒㄧㄣdon't worry

Examples

  • 我很紧张。wǒ hěn jǐnzhāng.I'm nervous.
  • 别担心！bié dānxīn!Don't worry!

Bored, tired, awkward

无聊 means both “bored” and “boring.”

无聊wúliáo無聊ㄨˊ ㄌㄧㄠˊbored; boring
累lèi累ㄌㄟˋtired
尴尬gāngà尷尬ㄍㄢ ㄍㄚˋawkward, embarrassing
想家xiǎngjiā想家ㄒㄧㄤˇ ㄐㄧㄚhomesick

Examples

  • 好无聊！hǎo wúliáo!So boring!
  • 好尴尬。hǎo gāngà.So awkward.

Checking in

你还好吗？ means “are you okay?”

怎么了？zěnme le?怎麼了？ㄗㄣˇ ˙ㄇㄜ ˙ㄌㄜ？what's wrong? what happened?Asks a friend who seems upset.
没事méi shì沒事ㄇㄟˊ ㄕˋit's nothing, I'm fine, no problemAlso a casual reply to 对不起 or 谢谢.
  • 有点 (yǒudiǎn) + feeling = "a bit," usually for negative feelings.

Examples

  • 你怎么了？ 我有点累。nǐ zěnme le? wǒ yǒudiǎn lèi.What's wrong? I'm a bit tired.
