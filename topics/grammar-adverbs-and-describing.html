1. Noun + 很 + adjective

Level
A1 #34
Keywords
很

很 is often just a link here, not really "very."

Structure Examples Notes Watch out Practice Sources

Structure

Noun + 很 + adjective

Examples

  • 他很高。tā hěn gāo.He's tall.
  • 我很好。wǒ hěn hǎo.I'm good.

Notes

  • 非常 means "extremely."

Watch out

  • No 是 before an adjective: 他很高, not ✗ 他是高.

Practice

The weather is hot.天气很热。tiānqì hěn rè.Say it, then tap to check

2. 太 + adjective + 了

Level
A1 #33
Keywords
了、太

"Too" as a complaint, or "so" as praise.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

太 + adjective + 了

Examples

  • 太好了！tài hǎo le!Great!
  • 天气太热了。tiānqì tài rè le.It's too hot.your notes

3. 都: all, both

Level
A1 #3
Keywords
都

都 goes after the subject, never at the start of the sentence.

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

Subject + 都 + verb

Examples

  • 我们都说英语。wǒmen dōu shuō Yīngyǔ.We all speak English.
  • 他们都在上海。tāmen dōu zài Shànghǎi.They're all in Shanghai.AllSet

Notes

  • After any time word: 我们今天都在家.
  • With two people, it means "both."

Practice

We're all students.我们都是学生。wǒmen dōu shì xuésheng.Say it, then tap to check

4. 也: also, too

Level
A1 #4
Keywords
也

也 goes before the verb, never at the end like English "too."

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

Subject + 也 + verb

Examples

  • 我也是学生。wǒ yě shì xuésheng.I'm a student too.AllSet
  • 我也不喜欢。wǒ yě bù xǐhuan.I don't like it either.

Notes

  • In negatives, 也 goes before 不 or 没: 我也不去 (I'm not going either).
  • With adjectives, 也 goes before 很: 他也很高.

Practice

Me too.我也是。wǒ yě shì.Say it, then tap to check

Sources

  • AllSet: The "also" adverb "ye"
  • Wording and unmarked examples: Claude

5. Where adverbs go

Level
Beyond A1

After the subject, before the verb: 我很喜欢这本书.

Structure Examples Notes Sources

Structure

Subject + adverb + verb

Examples

  • 我很喜欢这本书。wǒ hěn xǐhuan zhè běn shū.I really like this book.your notes
  • 可能他不来。kěnéng tā bù lái.Maybe he isn't coming.

Notes

  • Most adverbs can't come before the subject. 也许 and 可能 can: 可能他不来.

Sources

  • Your notes. Matches the 都 and 也 articles on AllSet.

6. Adjective + 地 + verb

Level
B1
Keywords
地

地 turns an adjective into an adverb, like "-ly."

Structure Examples Notes Sources

Structure

Adjective + 地 + verb

Examples

  • 清楚地说qīngchu de shuōspeak clearly
  • 他清清楚楚地说。tā qīngqīngchǔchǔ de shuō.He spoke very clearly.

Notes

  • Two-syllable adjectives can double first: 清清楚楚地说.
  • Not very common in speech. Plain adverbs don't need it.

7. How …? with 多 + adjective

Level
A2
Keywords
多

Asks about degree: 多高 (how tall), 多大 (how big, how old).

Structure Examples Notes Practice Sources

Structure

Subject + 多 + adjective？

Examples

  • 你多高？nǐ duō gāo?How tall are you?
  • 他女儿多大了？ 七岁。tā nǚ'ér duō dà le? qī suì.How old is his daughter? Seven.your notes

Notes

  • For a small child's age, 几岁 is more natural.

Practice

How old are you?你多大了？nǐ duō dà le?Say it, then tap to check
