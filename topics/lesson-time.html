1. Days and dates

Used for
Clock & calendarMaking plans
Keywords
号、年、日、月、今天、明天、星期、昨天

Days: 星期 + number. 星期一 is Monday … 星期六 is Saturday. Sunday is 星期天.

Key words Examples Notes Practice Sources

Key words

今天jīntiān今天ㄐㄧㄣ ㄊㄧㄢtoday
明天míngtiān明天ㄇㄧㄥˊ ㄊㄧㄢtomorrow
昨天zuótiān昨天ㄗㄨㄛˊ ㄊㄧㄢyesterdayLook-alike: 作 (zuò, as in 工作). 昨 has the sun radical 日.
下个月xià ge yuè下個月ㄒㄧㄚˋ ˙ㄍㄜ ㄩㄝˋnext month
星期xīngqī星期ㄒㄧㄥ ㄑㄧˊweek, day of the weekTaiwan says xīngqí.
星期天xīngqītiān星期天ㄒㄧㄥ ㄑㄧˊ ㄊㄧㄢSundayAlso 星期日. Taiwan says xīngqí.
月yuè月ㄩㄝˋmonth, moon
号hào號ㄏㄠˋday of the month今天几月几号？ asks the date.
日rì日ㄖˋday, sunFrom 年月日 (year, month, day).
年nián年ㄋㄧㄢˊyear
生日shēngrì生日ㄕㄥ ㄖˋbirthday

Examples

  • 今天星期几？ 今天星期五。jīntiān xīngqī jǐ? jīntiān xīngqīwǔ.What day is it? It's Friday.
  • 今天几月几号？jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào?What's the date today?
  • 我的生日是五月三号。wǒ de shēngrì shì wǔ yuè sān hào.My birthday is May 3rd.

Notes

  • Months: number + 月. 一月 is January … 十二月 is December.
  • Dates go big to small: 2026年11月9号.
  • 号 is spoken. 日 is written.
  • Taiwan says 星期 as xīngqí.

2. Clock time

Used for
Clock & calendarMaking plans
Keywords
分、时、点、秒、分钟、时候、现在

Number + 点 = o'clock. 2 o'clock is 两点, not 二点.

Key words Examples Notes Practice Sources

Key words

现在xiànzài現在ㄒㄧㄢˋ ㄗㄞˋnow
点diǎn點ㄉㄧㄢˇo’clock, point2 o’clock uses 两点.
分fēn分ㄈㄣminute
分钟fēnzhōng分鐘ㄈㄣ ㄓㄨㄥminute
时shí時ㄕˊhourFrom 时分秒 (hour, minute, second).
秒miǎo秒ㄇㄧㄠˇsecond (time)
时候shíhou時候ㄕˊ ˙ㄏㄡtime, moment

Examples

  • 现在几点？xiànzài jǐ diǎn?What time is it?
  • 现在三点二十分。xiànzài sān diǎn èrshí fēn.It's 3:20.
  • 等我五分钟。děng wǒ wǔ fēnzhōng.Give me five minutes.

Notes

  • 分 is minutes on a clock. 分钟 is a number of minutes.
  • 时 alone is formal; an hour of time is 小时 (xiǎoshí).
  • 时候 means a time or moment: 什么时候 (when).

Practice

It's two o'clock.现在两点。xiànzài liǎng diǎn.Say it, then tap to check

3. Parts of the day

Used for
Clock & calendarDaily routine
Keywords
上午、下午、中午、早上、晚上

早上 early morning · 上午 before noon · 中午 noon · 下午 afternoon · 晚上 evening.

Key words Examples Notes Practice Sources

Key words

早上zǎoshang早上ㄗㄠˇ ˙ㄕㄤearly morning
上午shàngwǔ上午ㄕㄤˋ ㄨˇmorningBefore noon. 早上 is earlier in the morning.
中午zhōngwǔ中午ㄓㄨㄥ ㄨˇnoon (about 11 am – 1 pm)
下午xiàwǔ下午ㄒㄧㄚˋ ㄨˇafternoon
晚上wǎnshang晚上ㄨㄢˇ ˙ㄕㄤevening, night

Examples

  • 我早上七点起床。wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng.I get up at 7 in the morning.
  • 我们下午见。wǒmen xiàwǔ jiàn.See you this afternoon.

Notes

  • Time words go before the verb: 我晚上看电视.

4. Making plans

Used for
Making plansReacting & agreeing
Keywords
可以、好啊、电影、走吧、等一下、一起去吧

吧 makes a suggestion: 走吧 (let's go).

Key words Examples Notes Practice Sources

Key words

走吧zǒu ba走吧ㄗㄡˇ ˙ㄅㄚlet's go吧 turns a sentence into a suggestion.
一起去吧yìqǐ qù ba一起去吧ㄧˋ ㄑㄧˇ ㄑㄩˋ ˙ㄅㄚlet's go together一起 is in Week 4 of the study plan.
好啊hǎo a好啊ㄏㄠˇ ˙ㄚsure, sounds goodA friendly yes to an invitation.
可以kěyǐ可以ㄎㄜˇ ㄧˇokay, that works; can (allowed)
等一下děng yíxià等一下ㄉㄥˇ ㄧˊ ㄒㄧㄚˋwait a moment一下 softens a verb: “a bit.”
电影diànyǐng電影ㄉㄧㄢˋ ㄧㄥˇmovie

Examples

  • 明天一起去看电影吧！ 好啊！míngtiān yìqǐ qù kàn diànyǐng ba! hǎo a!Let's go see a movie tomorrow! Sure!
  • 星期六可以吗？ 可以！xīngqīliù kěyǐ ma? kěyǐ!Does Saturday work? Yes!
  • 等一下，我来了！děng yíxià, wǒ lái le!One moment, I'm coming!

Notes

  • 一起 = together: 一起去吧.
  • Say yes with 好啊 (sure) or 可以 (that works).

Practice
