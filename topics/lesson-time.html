Time words go before the verb: 我明天去 (I'm going tomorrow).

Today, tomorrow, yesterday Days of the week Months and dates Practice Sources

Today, tomorrow, yesterday

天 means "day." 今天 today · 明天 tomorrow · 昨天 yesterday.

今天jīntiān今天ㄐㄧㄣ ㄊㄧㄢtoday
明天míngtiān明天ㄇㄧㄥˊ ㄊㄧㄢtomorrow
昨天zuótiān昨天ㄗㄨㄛˊ ㄊㄧㄢyesterdayLook-alike: 作 (zuò, as in 工作). 昨 has the sun radical 日.
现在xiànzài現在ㄒㄧㄢˋ ㄗㄞˋnow

Examples

  • 我今天很忙。wǒ jīntiān hěn máng.I'm busy today.

Days of the week

星期 + a number: 星期一 is Monday … 星期六 is Saturday. Sunday is 星期天. Taiwan says 星期 as xīngqí. 周末 is the weekend.

星期xīngqī星期ㄒㄧㄥ ㄑㄧˊweek, day of the weekTaiwan says xīngqí.
星期天xīngqītiān星期天ㄒㄧㄥ ㄑㄧˊ ㄊㄧㄢSundayAlso 星期日. Taiwan says xīngqí.
周末zhōumò週末ㄓㄡ ㄇㄛˋweekend

Examples

  • 今天星期几？ 今天星期五。jīntiān xīngqī jǐ? jīntiān xīngqīwǔ.What day is it? It's Friday.
  • 周末你做什么？zhōumò nǐ zuò shénme?What are you doing this weekend?

Months and dates

Months are number + 月: 一月 January … 十二月 December. Dates go big to small: year, month, day. 号 is spoken, 日 written.

月yuè月ㄩㄝˋmonth, moon
号hào號ㄏㄠˋday of the month今天几月几号？ asks the date.
日rì日ㄖˋday, sunFrom 年月日 (year, month, day).
年nián年ㄋㄧㄢˊyear
下个月xià ge yuè下個月ㄒㄧㄚˋ ˙ㄍㄜ ㄩㄝˋnext month
生日shēngrì生日ㄕㄥ ㄖˋbirthday

Examples

  • 今天几月几号？jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào?What's the date today?
  • 我的生日是五月三号。wǒ de shēngrì shì wǔ yuè sān hào.My birthday is May 3rd.

Practice

Telling the time

Number + 点 = o'clock. 2 o'clock is 两点, not 二点. 分 is minutes on a clock.

点diǎn點ㄉㄧㄢˇo’clock, point2 o’clock uses 两点.
分fēn分ㄈㄣminute
时shí時ㄕˊhourFrom 时分秒 (hour, minute, second).
秒miǎo秒ㄇㄧㄠˇsecond (time)

Examples

  • 现在几点？xiànzài jǐ diǎn?What time is it?
  • 现在三点二十分。xiànzài sān diǎn èrshí fēn.It's 3:20.

How long

分钟 is a number of minutes. An hour of time is 小时 (xiǎoshí); 时 alone is formal. 时候 means "a time, a moment."

分钟fēnzhōng分鐘ㄈㄣ ㄓㄨㄥminute
时候shíhou時候ㄕˊ ˙ㄏㄡtime, moment

Examples

  • 等我五分钟。děng wǒ wǔ fēnzhōng.Give me five minutes.

Practice

It's two o'clock.现在两点。xiànzài liǎng diǎn.Say it, then tap to check

Morning to night

早上 early morning · 上午 before noon · 中午 noon · 下午 afternoon · 晚上 evening.

早上zǎoshang早上ㄗㄠˇ ˙ㄕㄤearly morning
上午shàngwǔ上午ㄕㄤˋ ㄨˇmorningBefore noon. 早上 is earlier in the morning.
中午zhōngwǔ中午ㄓㄨㄥ ㄨˇnoon (about 11 am – 1 pm)
下午xiàwǔ下午ㄒㄧㄚˋ ㄨˇafternoon
晚上wǎnshang晚上ㄨㄢˇ ˙ㄕㄤevening, night

Examples

  • 我早上七点起床。wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng.I get up at 7 in the morning.
  • 我们下午见。wǒmen xiàwǔ jiàn.See you this afternoon.

Practice

4. Seasons and weather

Used for
WeatherClock & calendarDescribing things
Keywords
冷、热、下雨、下雪、冬天、凉快、刮风、夏天

天气怎么样？ asks about the weather. Weather words work like adjectives (很热) or verbs (下雨).

The four seasons Hot and cold Rain, snow and sun Practice Sources

The four seasons

Each season ends in 天: 春天, 夏天, 秋天, 冬天.

春天chūntiān春天ㄔㄨㄣ ㄊㄧㄢspring
夏天xiàtiān夏天ㄒㄧㄚˋ ㄊㄧㄢsummer
秋天qiūtiān秋天ㄑㄧㄡ ㄊㄧㄢautumn, fall
冬天dōngtiān冬天ㄉㄨㄥ ㄊㄧㄢwinter

Examples

  • 台湾的夏天很热。Táiwān de xiàtiān hěn rè.Taiwan's summers are hot.

Hot and cold

热 hot · 冷 cold · 凉快 pleasantly cool.

天气tiānqì天氣ㄊㄧㄢ ㄑㄧˋweather
热rè熱ㄖㄜˋhot (weather)
冷lěng冷ㄌㄥˇcold
凉快liángkuai涼快ㄌㄧㄤˊ ˙ㄎㄨㄞpleasantly cool

Examples

  • 今天天气怎么样？ 很热！jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng? hěn rè!How's the weather today? Really hot!

Rain, snow and sun

下雨 (rain) and 下雪 (snow) use 下, "to fall." 了 at the end means it has started: 下雨了.

下雨xià yǔ下雨ㄒㄧㄚˋ ㄩˇto rain
下雪xià xuě下雪ㄒㄧㄚˋ ㄒㄩㄝˇto snow
刮风guā fēng颳風ㄍㄨㄚ ㄈㄥto be windy
晴天qíngtiān晴天ㄑㄧㄥˊ ㄊㄧㄢsunny day

Examples

  • 下雨了。xià yǔ le.It's raining.
  • 今天是晴天。jīntiān shì qíngtiān.It's sunny today.

Practice

It's very cold today.今天很冷。jīntiān hěn lěng.Say it, then tap to check

Suggesting

吧 turns a sentence into a suggestion: 走吧 (let's go). 一起 means "together."

走吧zǒu ba走吧ㄗㄡˇ ˙ㄅㄚlet's go吧 turns a sentence into a suggestion.
一起去吧yìqǐ qù ba一起去吧ㄧˋ ㄑㄧˇ ㄑㄩˋ ˙ㄅㄚlet's go together一起 is in Week 4 of the study plan.
电影diànyǐng電影ㄉㄧㄢˋ ㄧㄥˇmovie

Examples

  • 明天一起去看电影吧！míngtiān yìqǐ qù kàn diànyǐng ba!Let's go see a movie tomorrow!

Saying yes

好啊 (sure) and 可以 (that works) agree to a plan. 等一下 is "wait a moment."

好啊hǎo a好啊ㄏㄠˇ ˙ㄚsure, sounds goodA friendly yes to an invitation.
可以kěyǐ可以ㄎㄜˇ ㄧˇokay, that works; can (allowed)
等一下děng yíxià等一下ㄉㄥˇ ㄧˊ ㄒㄧㄚˋwait a moment一下 softens a verb: “a bit.”
什么时候shénme shíhou什麼時候ㄕㄣˊ ˙ㄇㄜ ㄕˊ ˙ㄏㄡwhenPast or future: 你什么时候吃的？

Examples

  • 星期六可以吗？ 可以！xīngqīliù kěyǐ ma? kěyǐ!Does Saturday work? Yes!
