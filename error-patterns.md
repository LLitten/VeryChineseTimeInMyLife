# Error patterns

Mistakes seen across practice, the vocabulary exam and the learner's own notes. Use these to choose what to practice and which look-alikes to separate.

## Look-alike characters (write them side by side)
| Pair | Meanings | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| 少 / 小 | shǎo few / xiǎo small | 少 has the extra stroke. The learner typed 小 for "few." |
| 下 / 不 | xià down / bù not | A short vertical stroke separates them. The learner answered "not" for 下. |
| 住 / 谁 | zhù to live / shéi who | Different left parts (亻 vs 讠) and different right parts. The learner answered "who" for 住. |
| 昨 / 作 | zuó (as in 昨天) / zuò (as in 工作) | Written as 作天 in one practice sentence. |
| 啤 / 脾 | pí (beer) / pí (spleen) | 啤酒 has a mouth radical, not the spleen character. |
| 坐 / 做 | zuò sit / zuò do, make | Same sound. 坐 has two people on the ground. 做 has the person radical. |
| 字 / 子 | zì character / zi (as in 儿子) | The learner answered "child" for 字. |

## Meanings that get swapped
- **不客气 and 对不起:** the learner swapped "you're welcome" and "sorry." 不客气 is the reply to 谢谢. 对不起 is sorry. For "sorry" the learner also wrote 不好意思, which is real but closer to "excuse me" or "I'm embarrassed."
- **漂亮 and 高兴:** pretty and happy.
- **菜 and 米饭:** dish or vegetables, and cooked rice.
- **和 and 也:** "and, with" vs "also."
- **怎么样 and 怎么:** "how is it?" vs "how/why."
- **儿子, 女儿, 儿:** 儿 means child; 儿子 is son.
- **能 vs 可能:** 能 is "can, be able to"; 可能 is "possible, maybe."

## Words cut short or mistyped
- **电** instead of 电脑, **气** instead of 天气, **杯** instead of 杯子.
- **工死** instead of 工作, and **作天** instead of 昨天. When typing pinyin, check the candidate list before choosing.

## Grammar tendencies
- **English tense creeping in.** Mandarin has no tense, and the time word does the work. Example: 今天我不想去学校 is present ("I don't want to go today"), not past.
- **了 as past tense.** 了 marks completion or a change of situation. In 他就要来了 it points to the future ("about to").
- **要 vs 在 for the future.** 在 + verb means happening now. For plans, use a time word, with or without 要.
- **不 vs 没.** The learner now chooses these correctly most of the time. 没 for past, 不 for future and habits. Never combine 没 with 了.
- **学 vs 看.** 学 is for subjects and skills (学中文). 看 is for reading and looking (看书). The learner wrote 学书 twice.
- **都 and 也 placement.** Both go after the subject and any time word, before the verb phrase: 我们今天都在家.
- **Measure words.** 书 takes 本, not 个. After 没有 the number and measure word usually drop out.
- **Duration order.** Duration goes after the verb, before the object (学了两个小时数学).

## Typing and regional notes
- **一点 vs 一点儿.** Taiwan usually says 一點 without 儿. Northern mainland speakers add 儿.
- **哪里 vs 哪儿.** 哪裡 is common in Taiwan, 哪儿 in the north.
- **Reading vs writing.** The learner reads and writes simplified first. Traditional forms are recognition-only for now.

## What the learner does well
- Numbers, including the previously shaky 七 and 九.
- Basic word order and time words before the verb.
- Choosing 不 vs 没 and 要 vs 会 in short sentences.
- Quoting and reporting speech with 说 and 听…说.
- Leaving a word blank when they don't know it, which keeps wrong guesses from sticking.
