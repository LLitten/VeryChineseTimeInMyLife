# Study plan

About 30 minutes a day, mostly in the evening, plus a short daytime review. The plan is built around the exam gaps in `progress.md` and the habits in `AGENTS.md`.

## Principles
- Fill gaps; don't restart. Most misses were words not yet learned, not words mixed up.
- Practice new words inside frames the learner already knows (see the grammar lessons in `grammar/`).
- Space reviews: the next day, three days later, a week later.
- Consistency beats length. A 5-minute day beats a skipped day. Don't try to catch up.

## Daily routine (about 30 minutes)
- **10 min, vocabulary:** 6 to 8 new words plus review, spoken aloud.
- **10 min, grammar:** swap words into one frame.
- **10 min, reading or practice:** graded reader, or a short round with an AI model or friend.
- **Writing (about 10 min when time allows):** 5 characters from the day's words. Write from memory, check stroke order on strokeorder.com, recopy the ones missed.
- **Daytime touch (3 to 5 min):** quick review of last night's words, ideally aloud. This also builds recall away from the evening study context.

## Minimum day (5 minutes)
Review yesterday's words, or answer 5 questions.

## Weekly rhythm
- **One friend session:** prepare 3 phrases beforehand, try them, note what confused you.
- **End of week:** a recall quiz on that week's words.

## Four-week roadmap
**Week 1: verbs and look-alikes.** 读, 写, 开, 坐, 回, 做, 打电话. Fix 下/不, 少/小, 住/谁, and the swap of 不客气 and 对不起. Frames: 我喜欢 / 想 / 要 + verb.

**Week 2: people, places, directions.** 先生, 小姐, 同学, 医生, 商店, 出租车, 上, 里, 前面, 后面. Frames: 在 + place, 有 for "there is," questions with 哪儿 and 谁.

**Week 3: things, time, weather.** 衣服, 椅子, 桌子, 电视, 电影, 分钟, 号, 下雨. Frames: 的, 太…了, 不太, 呢, 怎么样, and 了 for completion vs change.

**Week 4: consolidate.** Retake the full exam. Longer sentences with friends. Start high-frequency words beyond HSK 1 such as 也, 要 and 一起.

## Checkpoints
- End of Week 2 and end of Week 4: retake the vocabulary exam and compare the topic table.
- Add a line to the session log in `progress.md` after each session.

## Who does what
- **Friends:** tones, listening, natural phrasing, regional differences.
- **AI models:** grammar explanations, practice rounds, grading, preparing phrases for friends. Verify anything important with a friend or reference.
- **Dictionary app and graded reader:** lookup and reading in context.
- **strokeorder.com:** stroke order and character parts.
- **AllSet Chinese Grammar Wiki and hsk.academy:** grammar pages and the 150-word list.
