# Actions in progress
summary: Saying something is happening right now with 在 and 正在.
intro: To say something is happening right now, put 在 before the verb. This page shows how, and when to add 正在 or 呢.
related: Home & life

## (正)在 + verb: is doing
tag: A2
pattern: Subject + (正)在 + verb (+ object) (+ 呢)
- 在 + verb = happening right now.
- 正在 stresses "right now." Plain 在 is more common.
- 呢 at the end adds a casual, ongoing feel.
- Don't use it for plans. For the future, use a time word or 要.
### Examples
- 她们在买衣服呢。 | tāmen zài mǎi yīfu ne. | They're buying clothes. | your notes
- 我在喝茶。 | wǒ zài hē chá. | I'm drinking tea.
- 我们正在上课。 | wǒmen zhèngzài shàngkè. | We're in class right now. | AllSet
### Try it
- try: I'm eating. | 我在吃饭。 | wǒ zài chīfàn.
- try: What are you doing? | 你在做什么？ | nǐ zài zuò shénme?
