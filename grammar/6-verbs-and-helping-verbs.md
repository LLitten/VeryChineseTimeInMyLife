# Verbs and helping verbs
summary: Want, can, may, have to, let, and verbs for names and going places.
related: Conversation; Out & about

## Want: 要, 想, 喜欢
tag: A1 #27
pattern: Subject + 要 / 想 / 喜欢 + verb
- 要 = want to. It can sound firm, like a plan.
- 想 = would like to. Softer, and negated with 不想.
- 喜欢 + verb = like doing.
### Examples
- 我要喝水。 | wǒ yào hē shuǐ. | I want water.
- 我想买水。 | wǒ xiǎng mǎi shuǐ. | I'd like to buy water.
- 我喜欢吃饭。 | wǒ xǐhuan chīfàn. | I like eating.
### Try it
- try: What do you want to eat? | 你要吃什么？ | nǐ yào chī shénme?

## Going to: 要
tag: A1 #25
pattern: Subject + time + 要 + verb
- For plans you expect to carry out.
- Use a time word, or 要 may sound like "want."
- Don't use 在 for the future. 在 + verb means right now.
### Examples
- 我明天要去北京。 | wǒ míngtiān yào qù Běijīng. | I'm going to Beijing tomorrow.
- 他就要来了。 | tā jiù yào lái le. | He's about to arrive.
### Try it
- try: I'm going to buy a phone tomorrow. | 我明天要买手机。 | wǒ míngtiān yào mǎi shǒujī.
source: AllSet: Expressing "be going to" with "yao" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGVT2KN

## Can: 会 and 能
tag: A1 #24 · #23
pattern: Subject + 会 / 能 + verb
- 会 = a learned skill: languages, driving, cooking. 不会 = don't know how.
- 会 also means "will": 明天会很好.
- 能 = able to, when circumstances allow. Also for polite requests: 我能…吗？
### Examples
- 我会说中文。 | wǒ huì shuō Zhōngwén. | I can speak Chinese.
- 我会画画。 | wǒ huì huàhuà. | I can draw. | your notes
- 我能问你一个问题吗？ | wǒ néng wèn nǐ yí ge wèntí ma? | Could I ask you a question? | AllSet
### Try it
- try: Can you speak English? | 你会说英语吗？ | nǐ huì shuō Yīngyǔ ma?
source: AllSet: Expressing a learned skill with "hui" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGRHM0E
source: AllSet: Expressing ability or possibility with "neng" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASG9PQ40

## May: 可以
tag: A1 #26
pattern: Subject + 可以 + verb
- Permission, like "may."
- 不可以 = not allowed.
### Examples
- 我可以坐这儿吗？ 可以。 | wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma? kěyǐ. | May I sit here? Sure.
- 这里不可以吃东西。 | zhèlǐ bù kěyǐ chī dōngxi. | No eating here.
### Try it
- try: May I come in? | 我可以进来吗？ | wǒ kěyǐ jìnlái ma?
source: AllSet: Expressing permission with "keyi" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASG6SPYK

## Have to: 得 · Don't need to: 不用
tag: B1
pattern: Subject + 得 + verb · Subject + 不用 + verb
- 得 here is said děi and means "have to."
- "Don't need to" is 不用, not 不得 and not 不要.
### Examples
- 我们得走了。 | wǒmen děi zǒu le. | We have to go. | AllSet
- 你不用来。 | nǐ búyòng lái. | You don't need to come.
### Try it
- try: I have to work. | 我得工作。 | wǒ děi gōngzuò.
source: AllSet: Expressing "must" with "dei" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/Expressing_%22must%22_with_%22dei%22
source: AllSet: Expressing "don't need to" with "buyong" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/Expressing_%22don%27t_need_to%22_with_%22buyong%22

## Let or make someone: 让
tag: B1
pattern: Subject + 让 + person + verb
- 让 = let, make, or ask someone to.
- 想让 = want someone to: 我想让你学习.
### Examples
- 他让我睡觉。 | tā ràng wǒ shuìjiào. | He lets me sleep.
- 你让我很高兴。 | nǐ ràng wǒ hěn gāoxìng. | You make me happy.
- 你为什么不让我去？ | nǐ wèishénme bú ràng wǒ qù? | Why won't you let me go? | AllSet
### Try it
- try: I want you to come. | 我想让你来。 | wǒ xiǎng ràng nǐ lái.
source: AllSet: Causative verbs | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/Causative_verbs

## Going places: 去
tag: A1 #21
pattern: Subject + 去 + place · Subject + 去 + verb
- No word for "to": 我去学校.
- No 在 after 去.
- 去 + verb = go to do something: 我去买东西.
### Examples
- 我明天去学校。 | wǒ míngtiān qù xuéxiào. | I'm going to school tomorrow.
- 我去买东西。 | wǒ qù mǎi dōngxi. | I'm going shopping.
### Try it
- try: I'm going to the bank. | 我去银行。 | wǒ qù yínháng.
source: AllSet: Using the verb "qu" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGFYNCL

## Names: 叫 and 姓
tag: A1 #20 · #22
pattern: Subject + 叫 + name · Subject + 姓 + surname
- 叫 = to be called. Works for people, pets and places.
- 姓 = surname. The polite question is 您贵姓？
### Examples
- 我叫安娜。你叫什么名字？ | wǒ jiào Ānnà. nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? | I'm Anna. What's your name?
- 我姓王。 | wǒ xìng Wáng. | My surname is Wang.
### Try it
- try: What's your name? | 你叫什么名字？ | nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
