## Building numbers
tag: A1 #11
pattern: Big to small: 二十二, 一百零五
- 11–19: 十 + number. Tens: number + 十.
- A zero in the middle is said once as 零: 一百零一 (101).
- In phone numbers, 1 is often yāo.
### Examples
- 二十二 | èrshí'èr | 22
- 三百零五 | sānbǎi líng wǔ | 305 | AllSet
### Try it
- try: 99 | 九十九 | jiǔshíjiǔ
source: AllSet: Structure of numbers | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGCRH6P

## 二 vs 两
tag: A1 #40
pattern: 二 for numbers · 两 + measure word
- 二 is the plain number: counting, 十二, 第二 (2nd), 二号 (the 2nd).
- 两 goes before a measure word: 两个人, 两本书.
- 2 o'clock is 两点.
### Examples
- 两个小时 | liǎng ge xiǎoshí | two hours | AllSet
- 十二 | shí'èr | 12
### Try it
- try: two people | 两个人 | liǎng ge rén
source: AllSet: Comparing "er" and "liang" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGJ46H1

## Measure words
tag: A1 #8
pattern: Number + measure word + noun
- Every counted noun needs a measure word.
- 个 is the most common, and works when you're unsure.
- With "one," 一 can drop: 我要个苹果.
- Common ones: 本 books · 杯 cups · 只 animals · 条 long things.
### Examples
- 一个人 | yí ge rén | one person
- 四杯牛奶 | sì bēi niúnǎi | four glasses of milk | AllSet
- 三本书 | sān běn shū | three books
### Try it
- try: two cups of tea | 两杯茶 | liǎng bēi chá

## Age with 岁
tag: A1 #7
pattern: Subject + number + 岁
- No 是 and no 个: 我二十五岁.
- 岁半 = and a half.
### Examples
- 他女儿七岁。 | tā nǚ'ér qī suì. | His daughter is seven. | your notes
- 我儿子一岁半。 | wǒ érzi yí suì bàn. | My son is one and a half.
### Try it
- try: I'm thirty. | 我三十岁。 | wǒ sānshí suì.
source: AllSet: Age with "sui" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGQUKJF

## Dates
tag: A1 #9
pattern: Year + 年 + month + 月 + day + 号 / 日
- Big to small, like addresses.
- 号 is spoken. 日 is written.
### Examples
- 今天几月几号？ | jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào? | What's the date today?
- 一月一日是新年。 | yī yuè yī rì shì Xīnnián. | January 1st is New Year's Day. | AllSet
### Try it
- try: My birthday is May 3rd. | 我的生日是五月三号。 | wǒ de shēngrì shì wǔ yuè sān hào.
source: AllSet: Structure of dates | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGT3KF6

## Days of the week
tag: A1 #10
pattern: 星期 + number
- 星期一 Monday … 星期六 Saturday. The week starts on Monday.
- Sunday is 星期天 or 星期日.
- 是 is optional: 今天星期五.
### Examples
- 今天星期几？ | jīntiān xīngqī jǐ? | What day is it today?
- 今天是星期一吗？ | jīntiān shì xīngqīyī ma? | Is today Monday? | AllSet
### Try it
- try: Tomorrow is Sunday. | 明天星期天。 | míngtiān xīngqītiān.
source: AllSet: Structure of days of the week | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGVH4Q5

## Clock time
tag: A1 #12
pattern: Number + 点 (+ number + 分) · number + 点半
- 两点 for 2 o'clock, not 二点.
- 半 = half past: 两点半.
- 上午 a.m., 下午 p.m.: 下午三点.
### Examples
- 现在十点。 | xiànzài shí diǎn. | It's 10 o'clock.
- 下午两点半 | xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn bàn | 2:30 p.m. | AllSet
### Try it
- try: It's 3:20. | 现在三点二十分。 | xiànzài sān diǎn èrshí fēn.
source: AllSet: Structure of times (basic) | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGN6BBU
