# Location and "there is"
summary: 在 for where things are, 有 for "there is" and "have", and direction words.
related: Out & about; Home & life

## Be somewhere: 在
tag: A1 #17
pattern: Subject + 在 + place
- 在 is the verb here. Don't add 是: ❌ 我是在家.
- Negate with 不: 他不在学校.
### Examples
- 我在家。 | wǒ zài jiā. | I'm at home.
- 你在哪儿？ | nǐ zài nǎr? | Where are you?
### Try it
- try: He isn't at school. | 他不在学校。 | tā bú zài xuéxiào.
source: AllSet: Expressing existence in a place with "zai" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGLRWT8

## There is: place + 有
tag: A1 #18
pattern: Place + 有 + thing
- The place goes first: "the table has a cup" = there's a cup on the table.
- Negative: 没有.
### Examples
- 桌子上有一个杯子。 | zhuōzi shàng yǒu yí ge bēizi. | There's a cup on the table. | your notes
- 附近有公园吗？ | fùjìn yǒu gōngyuán ma? | Is there a park nearby?
### Try it
- try: There are people in the room. | 房间里有人。 | fángjiān lǐ yǒu rén.
source: AllSet: Expressing existence with "you" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASG04ZCI

## Have: subject + 有
tag: A1 #19
pattern: Subject + 有 + thing
- "Have." Negative: 没有, never 不有.
### Examples
- 我有三个朋友。 | wǒ yǒu sān ge péngyou. | I have three friends.
- 我有钱。 | wǒ yǒu qián. | I have money. | AllSet
### Try it
- try: I have a dog. | 我有一只狗。 | wǒ yǒu yì zhī gǒu.
source: AllSet: Expressing possession with "you" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGOOCVO

## Exactly where: direction words
tag: A2
pattern: Subject + 在 + place + direction word
- The direction word goes after the place: 学校后边 (behind the school).
- Short 上 and 里 attach straight to the noun: 桌子上, 房间里.
### Examples
- 猫在桌子上面。 | māo zài zhuōzi shàngmiàn. | The cat is on the table.
- 商店在学校后边。 | shāngdiàn zài xuéxiào hòubiān. | The shop is behind the school.
### Try it
- try: The bank is next to the school. | 银行在学校旁边。 | yínháng zài xuéxiào pángbiān.
