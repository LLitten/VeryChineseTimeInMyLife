## Noun + 很 + adjective
tag: A1 #34
pattern: Noun + 很 + adjective
- No 是 before an adjective: 他很高, not ❌ 他是高.
- 很 is often just a link here, not really "very."
- 非常 means "extremely."
### Examples
- 他很高。 | tā hěn gāo. | He's tall.
- 我很好。 | wǒ hěn hǎo. | I'm good.
### Try it
- try: The weather is hot. | 天气很热。 | tiānqì hěn rè.
source: AllSet: Simple "noun + adjective" sentences | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASG17BLH

## 太 + adjective + 了
tag: A1 #33
pattern: 太 + adjective + 了
- "Too" as a complaint, or "so" as praise.
- Keep the 了.
### Examples
- 太好了！ | tài hǎo le! | Great!
- 天气太热了。 | tiānqì tài rè le. | It's too hot. | your notes
- 你太好了。 | nǐ tài hǎo le. | You're so great. | AllSet
### Try it
- try: Too expensive! | 太贵了！ | tài guì le!
source: AllSet: Expressing "excessively" with "tai" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASG8HVFN

## 都: all, both
tag: A1 #3
pattern: Subject + 都 + verb
- 都 goes after the subject, never at the start of the sentence.
- After any time word: 我们今天都在家.
- With two people, it means "both."
### Examples
- 我们都说英语。 | wǒmen dōu shuō Yīngyǔ. | We all speak English.
- 他们都在上海。 | tāmen dōu zài Shànghǎi. | They're all in Shanghai. | AllSet
### Try it
- try: We're all students. | 我们都是学生。 | wǒmen dōu shì xuésheng.
source: AllSet: The "all" adverb "dou" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASG9YK09

## 也: also, too
tag: A1 #4
pattern: Subject + 也 + verb
- 也 goes before the verb, never at the end like English "too."
- In negatives, 也 goes before 不 or 没: 我也不去 (I'm not going either).
- With adjectives, 也 goes before 很: 他也很高.
### Examples
- 我也是学生。 | wǒ yě shì xuésheng. | I'm a student too. | AllSet
- 我也不喜欢。 | wǒ yě bù xǐhuan. | I don't like it either.
### Try it
- try: Me too. | 我也是。 | wǒ yě shì.
source: AllSet: The "also" adverb "ye" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGG25MD

## Where adverbs go
tag: Beyond A1
pattern: Subject + adverb + verb
- After the subject, before the verb: 我很喜欢这本书.
- Most adverbs can't come before the subject. 也许 and 可能 can: 可能他不来.
### Examples
- 我很喜欢这本书。 | wǒ hěn xǐhuan zhè běn shū. | I really like this book. | your notes
- 可能他不来。 | kěnéng tā bù lái. | Maybe he isn't coming.
source: Your notes. Matches the 都 and 也 articles on AllSet.

## Adjective + 地 + verb
tag: B1
pattern: Adjective + 地 + verb
- 地 turns an adjective into an adverb, like "-ly."
- Two-syllable adjectives can double first: 清清楚楚地说.
- Not very common in speech. Plain adverbs don't need it.
### Examples
- 清楚地说 | qīngchu de shuō | speak clearly
- 他清清楚楚地说。 | tā qīngqīngchǔchǔ de shuō. | He spoke very clearly.
source: AllSet: Turning adjectives into adverbs | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/Turning_adjectives_into_adverbs
source: AllSet: Reduplication of adjectives | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/Reduplication_of_adjectives

## How …? with 多 + adjective
tag: A2
pattern: Subject + 多 + adjective？
- Asks about degree: 多高 (how tall), 多大 (how big, how old).
- For a small child's age, 几岁 is more natural.
### Examples
- 你多高？ | nǐ duō gāo? | How tall are you?
- 他女儿多大了？ 七岁。 | tā nǚ'ér duō dà le? qī suì. | How old is his daughter? Seven. | your notes
### Try it
- try: How old are you? | 你多大了？ | nǐ duō dà le?
