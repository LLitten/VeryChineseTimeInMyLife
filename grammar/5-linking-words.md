# Linking words
summary: 和, 还是 and 或者, and the two jobs of 的.
related: Building blocks; People

## 和: and, with
tag: A1 #5
pattern: Noun + 和 + noun
- Joins nouns only, not verbs or sentences.
- It isn't every English "and."
### Examples
- 我和朋友 | wǒ hé péngyou | my friend and I
- 咖啡和茶都很好喝。 | kāfēi hé chá dōu hěn hǎohē. | Coffee and tea are both good.
### Try it
- try: Mom and Dad | 妈妈和爸爸 | māma hé bàba
source: AllSet: Expressing "and" with "he" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGP0KFF

## Or: 还是 and 或者
tag: A1 #6
pattern: A + 还是 + B？ (questions) · A + 或者 + B (statements)
- 还是 is "or" in a question, when the listener should pick one.
- 或者 is "or" in a statement. It's an A2 point.
- Statements often end with 都可以 (either is fine).
### Examples
- 你想喝咖啡还是茶？ | nǐ xiǎng hē kāfēi háishì chá? | Do you want coffee or tea?
- 我喝咖啡或者茶，都可以。 | wǒ hē kāfēi huòzhě chá, dōu kěyǐ. | Coffee or tea, either is fine.
### Try it
- try: Today or tomorrow? | 今天还是明天？ | jīntiān háishì míngtiān?
source: AllSet: Offering choices with "haishi" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGA8NIB
source: AllSet: Expressing "or" in statements | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/Expressing_%22or%22_in_statements

## 的: possession
tag: A1 #14
pattern: Noun 1 + 的 + noun 2
- "Noun 1's noun 2": 我的书 (my book).
### Examples
- 我的书 | wǒ de shū | my book
- 老师的电脑 | lǎoshī de diànnǎo | the teacher's computer
### Try it
- try: my phone | 我的手机 | wǒ de shǒujī
source: AllSet: Expressing possession with "de" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGUHQD2

## Close people: no 的
tag: A1 #13
pattern: Pronoun + noun
- Drop 的 for family, close friends, partners, and your school or workplace.
- Keeping 的 there sounds distant.
### Examples
- 我妈妈 | wǒ māma | my mom
- 我朋友 | wǒ péngyou | my friend
- 我们学校 | wǒmen xuéxiào | our school
### Try it
- try: my dad | 我爸爸 | wǒ bàba
source: AllSet: Expressing close possession without "de" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGCI0KG

## 的: description + noun
tag: A2
pattern: Description + 的 + noun
- Links a description to a noun: 好吃的菜 (tasty food).
- Drop the noun to mean "the … one": 大的 (the big one).
### Examples
- 好吃的菜 | hǎochī de cài | tasty food
- 我要大的。 | wǒ yào dà de. | I want the big one.
