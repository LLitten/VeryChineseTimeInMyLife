# Sentence structure
summary: The order words go in: subject, time, place, verb, object.
related: Time; Out & about

## Basic order
tag: A1 #31
pattern: Subject + verb (+ object)
- Same order as English to start with: 我吃饭.
- Verbs never change form. 是 is "is," "am," "are" and "was."
- Things diverge from English once you add time, place or 也.
### Examples
- 我去学校。 | wǒ qù xuéxiào. | I go to school.
- 他喝茶。 | tā hē chá. | He drinks tea.
### Try it
- try: I eat. | 我吃饭。 | wǒ chīfàn.
source: AllSet: Basic sentence order | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGETNCO

## Time goes before the verb
tag: A2
pattern: Subject + time + verb, or time + subject + verb
- Time can go first or right after the subject. Both are correct.
- Not at the end, as in English: ❌ 我吃饭明天.
### Examples
- 我明天吃饭。 / 明天我吃饭。 | wǒ míngtiān chīfàn. / míngtiān wǒ chīfàn. | I'll eat tomorrow.
- 他今天不去。 | tā jīntiān bú qù. | He isn't going today.
### Try it
- try: I'm going tomorrow. | 我明天去。 | wǒ míngtiān qù.
source: AllSet: Time words and word order | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/Time_words_and_word_order

## Place goes after time, before the verb
tag: A1 #17
pattern: Subject + time + 在 + place + verb + object
- 在 + place comes before the action.
- Memory trick: you need time to get somewhere, and you need to be somewhere to do anything.
### Examples
- 我昨天在公司吃饭。 | wǒ zuótiān zài gōngsī chīfàn. | I ate at work yesterday.
- 我晚上在家看电视。 | wǒ wǎnshang zài jiā kàn diànshì. | I watch TV at home in the evening.
### Try it
- try: I study at school. | 我在学校学习。 | wǒ zài xuéxiào xuéxí.
source: AllSet: Expressing existence in a place with "zai" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGLRWT8
source: Memory trick: your notes

## Verbs with 在 + place after them
tag: A2
pattern: 住 / 坐 / 放 / 站 + 在 + place
- A few verbs put 在 + place after the verb: 住 (live), 坐 (sit), 放 (put), 站 (stand).
- Only these few. Don't say ❌ 我工作在公司.
- 坐 can also take a vehicle directly: 坐飞机.
### Examples
- 我住在北京。 | wǒ zhù zài Běijīng. | I live in Beijing.
- 你坐在这儿吧。 | nǐ zuò zài zhèr ba. | Sit here.
### Try it
- try: Where do you live? | 你住在哪里？ | nǐ zhù zài nǎlǐ?
source: AllSet: Special cases of "zai" following verbs | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/Special_cases_of_%22zai%22_following_verbs

## How long: duration after the verb
tag: A2
pattern: Subject + verb + 了 + duration + object
- The time span goes after the verb and before the object.
- There's no word for "for" in "for two hours."
- 的 can go between the duration and the object: 学了两个小时的数学.
### Examples
- 我学了两个小时数学。 | wǒ xué le liǎng ge xiǎoshí shùxué. | I studied math for two hours.
- 他学了一年中文。 | tā xué le yì nián Zhōngwén. | He studied Chinese for a year. | AllSet
### Try it
- try: I slept for eight hours. | 我睡了八个小时。 | wǒ shuì le bā ge xiǎoshí.
source: AllSet: Expressing duration with "le" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/Expressing_duration_with_%22le%22

## Putting it all together
tag: Beyond A1
pattern: Subject + time + manner + place + tool + with whom + verb + object
- A guideline for long sentences. The middle pieces shift in real speech.
- Start with who and when, end with the action.
### Examples
- 我昨天在学校用电脑跟朋友学数学。 | wǒ zuótiān zài xuéxiào yòng diànnǎo gēn péngyou xué shùxué. | Yesterday I studied math at school on a computer with my friend.

## Order inside a noun phrase
tag: Beyond A1
pattern: Possessive + this/that/number + measure word + adjective + noun
- Big picture first, the thing itself last.
### Examples
- 我的两本新书 | wǒ de liǎng běn xīn shū | my two new books
- 那个大杯子 | nàge dà bēizi | that big cup
source: Your notes

## "It" is usually dropped
tag: Beyond A1
- Chinese rarely has a word for "it." Say what you mean directly.
### Examples
- 现在五点。 | xiànzài wǔ diǎn. | It's 5 o'clock.
- 下雨了。 | xià yǔ le. | It's raining.
- 很好吃！ | hěn hǎochī! | It's delicious!
source: Your notes

## 是 links two nouns
tag: A1 #32
pattern: Noun 1 + 是 + noun 2
- 是 links two nouns: 我是学生.
- Not used before an adjective: ❌ 他是高.
### Examples
- 我是学生。 | wǒ shì xuésheng. | I'm a student.
- 那个人是谁？ | nàge rén shì shéi? | Who is that person? | your notes
### Try it
- try: She's a teacher. | 她是老师。 | tā shì lǎoshī.
source: AllSet: Connecting nouns with "shi" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGUN7RX
