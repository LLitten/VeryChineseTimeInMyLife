## Questions and answers
- Each pair mixes a few patterns from the other grammar lessons.
### Examples
- 那个人是谁？ 我不认识她。 | nàge rén shì shéi? wǒ bú rènshi tā. | Who is that person? I don't know her. | your notes
- 你喝水吗？ 好的，谢谢。 | nǐ hē shuǐ ma? hǎo de, xièxie. | Would you like water? Okay, thanks. | your notes
- 他女儿多大了？ 七岁。 | tā nǚ'ér duō dà le? qī suì. | How old is his daughter? Seven. | your notes
- 你的同学在哪儿工作？ 医院。 | nǐ de tóngxué zài nǎr gōngzuò? yīyuàn. | Where does your classmate work? At a hospital. | your notes
- 昨天上午天气怎么样？ 下雨了。 | zuótiān shàngwǔ tiānqì zěnmeyàng? xià yǔ le. | What was the weather like yesterday morning? It rained. | your notes
- 爸爸什么时候来北京呢？ 下个月。 | bàba shénme shíhou lái Běijīng ne? xià ge yuè. | When is Dad coming to Beijing? Next month. | your notes

## Everyday sentences
- 睡觉了 means "gone to sleep" (a change), not "sleeping."
- 多吃些 means "eat more," not "eat many."
- 一块儿 usually means "together." For "a piece," say 一块.
