# Body & health
summary: Parts of the body, saying what hurts, and getting sick.
related: Feelings; Home & life; Grammar: Adverbs and describing

## The body
身体 means "body," and also "health": 你身体好吗？ asks how someone's health is.
### Head and face
words: 头 [noun], 头发, 眼睛, 鼻子, 耳朵, 嘴, 牙齿
- 她的眼睛很大。 | tā de yǎnjing hěn dà. | She has big eyes.
- 他的头发很长。 | tā de tóufa hěn cháng. | His hair is long.
### Body
words: 身体, 手, 脚, 肚子
- 我的手很冷。 | wǒ de shǒu hěn lěng. | My hands are cold.
### Try it
- try: My eyes are tired. | 我的眼睛很累。 | wǒ de yǎnjing hěn lèi.

## Saying what hurts
Body part + 疼 = it hurts: 头疼 (headache), 肚子疼 (stomachache). In Taiwan, 痛 (tòng) is more common: 頭痛.
### Pain
words: 疼
- 我头疼。 | wǒ tóu téng. | I have a headache.
- 我肚子疼。 | wǒ dùzi téng. | My stomach hurts.
### Feeling unwell
舒服 is "comfortable." 不舒服 is the usual way to say you feel unwell. 累 is "tired."
words: 舒服, 累
- 我不舒服。 | wǒ bù shūfu. | I don't feel well.
- 我今天很累。 | wǒ jīntiān hěn lèi. | I'm really tired today.
### Try it
- try: I have a headache. | 我头疼。 | wǒ tóu téng.
- try: I don't feel well. | 我不舒服。 | wǒ bù shūfu.

## Getting sick
### Being sick
生病 is "get sick." 感冒 is a cold; 我感冒了 means "I've caught a cold."
words: 生病, 感冒
- 我感冒了。 | wǒ gǎnmào le. | I've got a cold.
- 他生病了。 | tā shēngbìng le. | He's sick.
### Getting better
You 吃药 (eat medicine) to take medicine, and 看医生 to see a doctor.
words: 药, 看医生, 医生, 医院
- 你要吃药。 | nǐ yào chī yào. | You need to take some medicine.
- 我明天去看医生。 | wǒ míngtiān qù kàn yīshēng. | I'm seeing a doctor tomorrow.
### Staying healthy
运动 is "exercise" or "sports."
words: 运动
- 我每天运动。 | wǒ měi tiān yùndòng. | I exercise every day.
### Try it
- try: I've caught a cold. | 我感冒了。 | wǒ gǎnmào le.
- try: Take some medicine. | 吃药吧。 | chī yào ba.
