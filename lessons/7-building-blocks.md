## People words
### I, you, he, she
他 and 她 sound the same (tā); only the writing differs. Add 们 for more than one person.
words: 我, 你, 他, 她, 们, 我们, 她们
- 我们是朋友。 | wǒmen shì péngyou. | We're friends.
### This and that
这 is this, 那 is that. Before a noun they need a measure word: 这个, 那本.
words: 这, 那
- 这是我的，那是你的。 | zhè shì wǒ de, nà shì nǐ de. | This is mine; that's yours.
### Try it
- try: They're students. | 他们是学生。 | tāmen shì xuésheng.

## Numbers
Chinese numbers build up very regularly.
### One to ten, and beyond
十一 is "ten-one" (11), 二十 "two-ten" (20), 二十二 (22). 百 is a hundred.
words: 零, 一, 二, 三, 四, 五, 六, 七, 八, 九, 十, 百
- 三十五 | sānshíwǔ | 35
### 二 or 两
二 is for counting and numbers. 两 goes before a measure word: 两个人.
words: 两
- 我有两个朋友。 | wǒ yǒu liǎng ge péngyou. | I have two friends.
### Tone changes in 一
一 is yī alone, yí before a 4th tone (一个 yí ge), and yì before the others (一起 yìqǐ).
### Try it
- try: 99 | 九十九 | jiǔshíjiǔ
- try: two books | 两本书 | liǎng běn shū

## Measure words
Every counted noun needs a measure word: number + measure word + noun.
### The everyday ones
个 works for most things when you're not sure. 些 means "some."
words: 个, 些
- 我要一些水果。 | wǒ yào yìxiē shuǐguǒ. | I'd like some fruit.
### For people
位 is the polite one: 一位老师. 名 is used for people in a role, and 口 for family members.
words: 位, 名, 口
- 一位老师 | yí wèi lǎoshī | a teacher (polite)
### More measure words
Measure words for food, drinks, animals and furniture are in those lessons.

## Describing things
Describing words (adjectives) don't need 是. Use 很 instead: 他很高.
### Very, too and not very
很 very · 非常 extremely · 太…了 too / so · 不太 not very (no 了).
words: 很, 非常, 太, 不太, 一点儿
- 这个太大了。 | zhège tài dà le. | This is too big.
- 我不太高兴。 | wǒ bú tài gāoxìng. | I'm not very happy.
### Common describing words
words: 好, 大, 小, 多, 少, 漂亮, 高兴, 清楚
- 她很漂亮。 | tā hěn piàoliang. | She's pretty.
### Opposites
Learning words in pairs helps them stick.
words: 高, 矮, 长, 短, 胖, 瘦, 新, 旧, 快, 慢, 贵, 便宜, 远, 近, 难, 容易, 忙
- 中文不难！ | Zhōngwén bù nán! | Chinese isn't hard!
- 太慢了。 | tài màn le. | Too slow.
### Try it
- try: It's very small. | 很小。 | hěn xiǎo.
- try: It's cheap. | 很便宜。 | hěn piányi.

## Grammar glue
These small words appear everywhere. Each has a grammar lesson; see the links at the end.
### Linking words
的 (possession, description) · 和 (and, for nouns) · 还是 / 或者 (or).
words: 的, 地, 和, 还是, 或者
- 我和朋友 | wǒ hé péngyou | my friend and I
### Saying no
不 is for now, the future and habits. 没 is for the past and for 有. 别 / 不要 mean "don't."
words: 不, 没, 没有, 别, 不要, 不用
- 我昨天没去。 | wǒ zuótiān méi qù. | I didn't go yesterday.
### Also, all, and other helpers
也 and 都 go before the verb, never at the end. 了 marks a completed action or a change.
words: 也, 都, 也都, 了, 是, 有, 在, 会, 能, 得, 让, 用, 到
- 我们都会说英语。 | wǒmen dōu huì shuō Yīngyǔ. | We can all speak English.
