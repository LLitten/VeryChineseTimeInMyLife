# Building blocks
summary: Pronouns, numbers, measure words, describing words, and the small words that hold sentences together.
related: Grammar: Negation; Grammar: Adverbs and describing; Grammar: Linking words

## People words
words: 我, 你, 他, 她, 们, 我们, 她们, 这, 那
- Add 们 for more than one person: 我们, 你们, 他们.
- 他 and 她 sound the same: tā.
- 这 = this, 那 = that. Before a measure word: 这个, 那本.
### Examples
- 我们是朋友。 | wǒmen shì péngyou. | We're friends.
- 这是我的，那是你的。 | zhè shì wǒ de, nà shì nǐ de. | This is mine; that's yours.
### Try it
- try: They're students. | 他们是学生。 | tāmen shì xuésheng.

## Numbers
words: 零, 一, 二, 两, 三, 四, 五, 六, 七, 八, 九, 十, 百
- Build up: 十一 11 · 二十 20 · 二十二 22 · 一百 100.
- 二 is for counting. 两 goes before a measure word: 两个人.
- 一 changes tone: yī alone, yí before a 4th tone, yì before the others.
### Examples
- 我有两个朋友。 | wǒ yǒu liǎng ge péngyou. | I have two friends.
- 三十五 | sānshíwǔ | 35
### Try it
- try: 99 | 九十九 | jiǔshíjiǔ
- try: two books | 两本书 | liǎng běn shū

## Measure words
words: 个, 些, 位, 名, 口
- Number + measure word + noun: 三个人.
- 个 works for most things when you're not sure.
- 位 is the polite one for people: 一位老师.
- 些 = some: 这些, 一些.
- Measure words for food, drinks, animals and furniture are in those lessons.
### Examples
- 一位老师 | yí wèi lǎoshī | a teacher (polite)
- 我要一些水果。 | wǒ yào yìxiē shuǐguǒ. | I'd like some fruit.

## Describing things
words: 很, 非常, 太, 不太, 大, 小, 高, 好, 少, 多, 漂亮, 高兴, 清楚, 一点儿
- Noun + 很 + adjective, with no 是: 他很高.
- 太…了 = too, or so: 太好了！
- 不太 = not very. No 了.
- 一点儿 = a little. Taiwan says 一点.
### Examples
- 她很漂亮。 | tā hěn piàoliang. | She's pretty.
- 这个太大了。 | zhège tài dà le. | This is too big.
- 我不太高兴。 | wǒ bú tài gāoxìng. | I'm not very happy.
### Try it
- try: It's very small. | 很小。 | hěn xiǎo.

## Grammar glue
words: 是, 有, 在, 的, 了, 地, 和, 也, 都, 也都, 还是, 或者, 不, 没, 没有, 别, 不要, 不用, 得, 会, 能, 让, 用, 到
- These words each have a grammar page. See the links at the end.
- 不 is for now, the future and habits. 没 is for the past.
- 也 and 都 go before the verb, never at the end.
### Examples
- 我也喜欢。 | wǒ yě xǐhuan. | I like it too.
- 我昨天没去。 | wǒ zuótiān méi qù. | I didn't go yesterday.
- 我们都会说英语。 | wǒmen dōu huì shuō Yīngyǔ. | We can all speak English.
