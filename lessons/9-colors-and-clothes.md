# Colors & clothes
summary: Colors, what you're wearing, and describing how things look.
related: Out & about; Building blocks; Grammar: Linking words

## Colors
Most colors are a color word + 色 (color): 红色, 蓝色. In everyday speech 色 is often dropped: 红的 (the red one).
### Basic colors
words: 颜色, 红色, 黑色, 白色, 蓝色, 绿色, 黄色
- 你喜欢什么颜色？ | nǐ xǐhuan shénme yánsè? | What color do you like?
- 我喜欢蓝色。 | wǒ xǐhuan lánsè. | I like blue.
### More colors
Brown is 咖啡色, "coffee color." Orange is 橙色 on the mainland but usually 橘色 (júsè) in Taiwan.
words: 粉红色, 橙色, 紫色, 灰色, 咖啡色
- 这个是咖啡色的。 | zhège shì kāfēisè de. | This one is brown.
### Light and dark
深 (dark, deep) and 浅 (light) go in front: 深蓝色 navy, 浅绿色 light green.
words: 深, 浅
- 我要浅蓝色的。 | wǒ yào qiǎnlánsè de. | I want the light blue one.
### Colors and meanings
红色 means luck and celebration: red envelopes (红包) hold money for New Year. Be careful with 黄色: in slang it can mean "dirty" or "adult."
### Try it
- try: What color is this? | 这是什么颜色？ | zhè shì shénme yánsè?
- try: I like red. | 我喜欢红色。 | wǒ xǐhuan hóngsè.
source: LTL: Colors in Chinese | https://ltl-taiwan.com/chinese-colors/

## Clothes
### Wearing things
Chinese has two verbs for "wear." 穿 is for clothes and shoes. 戴 is for things you put on: hats, glasses, watches.
words: 穿, 戴
- 他穿白色的衬衫。 | tā chuān báisè de chènshān. | He's wearing a white shirt.
- 她戴帽子。 | tā dài màozi. | She's wearing a hat.
### Tops and jackets
衣服 means "clothes" in general. 外套 is any jacket or coat.
words: 衣服, 衬衫, T恤, 外套
- 今天很冷，穿外套吧。 | jīntiān hěn lěng, chuān wàitào ba. | It's cold today, wear a jacket.
### Pants, skirts and shoes
Pants words end in 裤: 牛仔裤 jeans, 短裤 shorts.
words: 裤子, 牛仔裤, 短裤, 裙子, 鞋子, 帽子
- 我想买一双鞋子。 | wǒ xiǎng mǎi yì shuāng xiézi. | I want to buy a pair of shoes.
- 双 (shuāng) is the measure word for a pair.
### Try it
- try: I'm wearing jeans. | 我穿牛仔裤。 | wǒ chuān niúzǎikù.
- try: He's wearing a hat. | 他戴帽子。 | tā dài màozi.
source: LTL: Clothes in Chinese | https://ltl-beijing.com/clothes-in-chinese

## Describing how things look
Put a color before 的 to describe something, then the thing: 红色的衣服 (red clothes).
### Color + 的 + thing
Drop the thing to mean "the … one": 我要红色的 (I want the red one).
- 我喜欢黑色的鞋子。 | wǒ xǐhuan hēisè de xiézi. | I like black shoes.
- 我要白色的。 | wǒ yào báisè de. | I'll take the white one.
### Try it
- try: I want the blue one. | 我要蓝色的。 | wǒ yào lánsè de.
