# Art & drawing
summary: Drawing and sketching, shapes, light and color, form and composition, kinds of art, and tools.
intro: These words help you talk about making art, from a quick sketch to a finished illustration, and about why a picture works: its shapes, light, color and composition. Many art words share the character 画 (huà, draw, picture), which makes them easier to recognize.
related: Game development; Colors & clothes; Jobs & free time

## Drawing
画 is both "to draw" and "a picture." 画画 is "to draw (pictures)" as an activity.
### Drawing and sketching
A 草图 is a rough first sketch; 素描 is a sketch or drawing study, often in pencil.
words: 画, 画画, 素描, 草图, 线条, 线
- 我喜欢画画。 | wǒ xǐhuan huàhuà. | I like to draw.
- 我先画草图。 | wǒ xiān huà cǎotú. | I'll do a rough sketch first.
### Coloring in
上色 comes after the linework is done.
words: 上色, 颜料, 水彩
- 我还没上色。 | wǒ hái méi shàngsè. | I haven't colored it yet.
- 我喜欢用水彩。 | wǒ xǐhuan yòng shuǐcǎi. | I like using watercolor.
### Try it
- try: I like to draw. | 我喜欢画画。 | wǒ xǐhuan huàhuà.
- try: I haven't colored it yet. | 我还没上色。 | wǒ hái méi shàngsè.

## Shapes
Shape names end in 形 (shape), so you can often guess them from their parts: 三角形 is "three-corner shape."
### Basic shapes
words: 形状, 圆形, 正方形, 长方形, 三角形
- 这是什么形状？ | zhè shì shénme xíngzhuàng? | What shape is this?
- 他的脸是圆形的。 | tā de liǎn shì yuánxíng de. | His face is round.
### Try it
- try: a triangle | 三角形 | sānjiǎoxíng
- try: What shape is this? | 这是什么形状？ | zhè shì shénme xíngzhuàng?

## Light, value and color
### Light and shadow
阴影 is shading on the object itself; 影子 is the shadow it casts on the ground.
words: 光, 亮, 暗, 高光, 阴影, 影子
- 这边太暗了。 | zhè biān tài àn le. | This side is too dark.
- 加一点高光。 | jiā yìdiǎn gāoguāng. | Add a bit of highlight.
### Warm and cool colors
Color names are in the Colors & clothes lesson.
words: 颜色, 暖色, 冷色, 对比, 深, 浅
- 背景用冷色。 | bèijǐng yòng lěngsè. | Use cool colors for the background.
- 对比不够。 | duìbǐ bú gòu. | There's not enough contrast.
### Try it
- try: This side is too dark. | 这边太暗了。 | zhè biān tài àn le.

## Form and composition
构图 is how a picture is arranged: what goes where, and what the eye sees first.
### Composition and balance
words: 构图, 平衡, 对称, 前景, 背景
- 这张画的构图很好。 | zhè zhāng huà de gòutú hěn hǎo. | This picture's composition is great.
- 前景太满了。 | qiánjǐng tài mǎn le. | The foreground is too busy.
### Proportion and perspective
比例 is proportion (a head too big for its body is 比例不对). 透视 is perspective: things far away look smaller.
words: 比例, 透视
- 比例不对。 | bǐlì bú duì. | The proportions are off.
### 2D, 3D and surface
平面 is flat or 2D; 立体 is three-dimensional, or looking solid. 质感 is how a surface looks like it would feel.
words: 平面, 立体, 轮廓, 质感
- 这个角色很立体。 | zhège juésè hěn lìtǐ. | This character looks really three-dimensional.
- 先画轮廓。 | xiān huà lúnkuò. | Draw the outline first.
### Try it
- try: The proportions are off. | 比例不对。 | bǐlì bú duì.
- try: Draw the outline first. | 先画轮廓。 | xiān huà lúnkuò.

## Kinds of art
### Art in general
艺术 is "art" in the broad sense; 美术 is visual art, and also the art team at a game studio.
words: 艺术, 美术, 画家, 作品, 设计
- 这是我的作品。 | zhè shì wǒ de zuòpǐn. | This is my work.
- 他是画家。 | tā shì huàjiā. | He's a painter.
### Illustration, comics and animation
words: 插画, 漫画, 动画
- 我画插画。 | wǒ huà chāhuà. | I draw illustrations.
- 你看漫画吗？ | nǐ kàn mànhuà ma? | Do you read comics?
### Try it
- try: This is my work. | 这是我的作品。 | zhè shì wǒ de zuòpǐn.
- try: Do you read comics? | 你看漫画吗？ | nǐ kàn mànhuà ma?

## Art tools
### Pens, pencils and paper
笔 is any pen or brush; 铅笔 ("lead pen") is a pencil.
words: 笔, 铅笔, 纸, 电脑
- 我用铅笔画素描。 | wǒ yòng qiānbǐ huà sùmiáo. | I sketch with a pencil.
- 我在电脑上画画。 | wǒ zài diànnǎo shàng huàhuà. | I draw on the computer.
### Try it
- try: I draw on the computer. | 我在电脑上画画。 | wǒ zài diànnǎo shàng huàhuà.
