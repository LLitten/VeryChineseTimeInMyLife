# Art & drawing
summary: Drawing and sketching, color, kinds of art, and art tools.
intro: These words help you talk about making art, whether it's a quick sketch or a finished illustration. Many art words share the character 画 (huà, draw, picture), which makes them easier to recognize.
related: Game development; Colors & clothes; Jobs & free time

## Drawing
画 is both "to draw" and "a picture." 画画 is "to draw (pictures)" as an activity.
### Drawing and sketching
A 草图 is a rough first sketch; 素描 is a sketch or drawing study, often in pencil.
words: 画, 画画, 素描, 草图, 线条
- 我喜欢画画。 | wǒ xǐhuan huàhuà. | I like to draw.
- 我先画草图。 | wǒ xiān huà cǎotú. | I'll do a rough sketch first.
### Color
上色 is "to color in," after the linework is done.
words: 上色, 颜色, 颜料, 水彩
- 我还没上色。 | wǒ hái méi shàngsè. | I haven't colored it yet.
- 我喜欢用水彩。 | wǒ xǐhuan yòng shuǐcǎi. | I like using watercolor.
### Try it
- try: I like to draw. | 我喜欢画画。 | wǒ xǐhuan huàhuà.
- try: I haven't colored it yet. | 我还没上色。 | wǒ hái méi shàngsè.

## Kinds of art
### Art in general
艺术 is "art" in the broad sense; 美术 is visual art, and also the art team at a game studio.
words: 艺术, 美术, 画家, 作品, 设计
- 这是我的作品。 | zhè shì wǒ de zuòpǐn. | This is my work.
- 他是画家。 | tā shì huàjiā. | He's a painter.
### Illustration, comics and animation
words: 插画, 漫画, 动画
- 我画插画。 | wǒ huà chāhuà. | I draw illustrations.
- 你看漫画吗？ | nǐ kàn mànhuà ma? | Do you read comics?
### Try it
- try: This is my work. | 这是我的作品。 | zhè shì wǒ de zuòpǐn.
- try: Do you read comics? | 你看漫画吗？ | nǐ kàn mànhuà ma?

## Art tools
### Pens, pencils and paper
笔 is any pen or brush; 铅笔 ("lead pen") is a pencil.
words: 笔, 铅笔, 纸, 电脑
- 我用铅笔画素描。 | wǒ yòng qiānbǐ huà sùmiáo. | I sketch with a pencil.
- 我在电脑上画画。 | wǒ zài diànnǎo shàng huàhuà. | I draw on the computer.
### Try it
- try: I draw on the computer. | 我在电脑上画画。 | wǒ zài diànnǎo shàng huàhuà.
