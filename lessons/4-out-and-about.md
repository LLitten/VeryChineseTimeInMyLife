# Out & about
summary: Places in town, directions, getting around, and shopping.
intro: These words help you find your way and get things done in town: naming places, giving and following directions, taking transport, and buying things. Taiwan-specific transport words are included.
related: Food & drink; Colors & clothes; Grammar: Location and "there is"; Grammar: Verbs and helping verbs

## Places in town
Two verbs cover most sentences about places: 在 (be at) and 去 (go to).
### Shops and services
words: 商店, 超市, 银行, 医院
- 我去超市买东西。 | wǒ qù chāoshì mǎi dōngxi. | I'm going to the supermarket.
- 他在医院工作。 | tā zài yīyuàn gōngzuò. | He works at a hospital.
### Around town
words: 学校, 公司, 公园, 酒店, 机场, 厕所, 北京
- 厕所在哪里？ | cèsuǒ zài nǎlǐ? | Where's the restroom?
### Signs
Mostly seen on signs, as in 入口 (entrance). In speech, use 进.
words: 入
### Try it
- try: Where's the bank? | 银行在哪里？ | yínháng zài nǎlǐ?
- try: I'm going to school. | 我去学校。 | wǒ qù xuéxiào.

## Directions
Say where something is with: thing + 在 + place + direction word.
### Left, right, front, back
The 边 and 面 versions mean the same: 前边 = 前面.
words: 左边, 右边, 前面, 后面, 前边, 后边
- 厕所在左边。 | cèsuǒ zài zuǒbiān. | The restroom is on the left.
- 学校在医院后面。 | xuéxiào zài yīyuàn hòumiàn. | The school is behind the hospital.
### In, out, on, under
Short 上 and 里 attach straight to a noun: 桌子上 (on the table), 家里 (at home).
words: 上, 下, 上面, 下面, 里, 里面, 外面, 向上, 向下
- 书在桌子上。 | shū zài zhuōzi shàng. | The book is on the table.
### Next to, across, between
words: 旁边, 对面, 中间, 周围, 角落, 尽头, 对角线
- 银行在超市对面。 | yínháng zài chāoshì duìmiàn. | The bank is across from the supermarket.
### Near and far
离 + place + 远 / 近: 离这里很近 (it's close to here).
words: 附近, 远, 近
- 附近有公园吗？ | fùjìn yǒu gōngyuán ma? | Is there a park nearby?
- 很近！ | hěn jìn! | It's close!
### Compass points
东西 (“east-west”) also means “things.”
words: 北, 南, 东, 西
### Finding things
words: 找, 找到, 找不到
- 我找不到我的书。 | wǒ zhǎo bu dào wǒ de shū. | I can't find my book.
### Try it
- try: The school is behind the hospital. | 学校在医院后面。 | xuéxiào zài yīyuàn hòumiàn.
- try: I can't find it. | 我找不到。 | wǒ zhǎo bu dào.
source: AllSet: Expressing existence with "you" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASG04ZCI

## Getting around
### Coming and going
来 is toward the speaker, 去 away from them.
words: 来, 去, 走, 回, 到
- 你什么时候回家？ | nǐ shénme shíhou huí jiā? | When are you going home?
- 我到了！ | wǒ dào le! | I'm here!
### Taking transport
坐 + vehicle = take it: 坐飞机. Scooters and bikes use 骑 (qí, ride).
words: 坐, 开, 飞机, 出租车, 司机
- 我们坐出租车吧。 | wǒmen zuò chūzūchē ba. | Let's take a taxi.
### Taiwan transport words
Taiwan has its own words for these.
words: 计程车, 公车, 捷运, 机车, 脚踏车
- 我坐捷运去公司。 | wǒ zuò jiéyùn qù gōngsī. | I take the MRT to work.
### Try it
- try: I take the bus to school. | 我坐公车去学校。 | wǒ zuò gōngchē qù xuéxiào.
- try: Let's go home. | 我们回家吧。 | wǒmen huí jiā ba.
source: AllSet: Using the verb "qu" | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ASGFYNCL

## Shopping and money
### Asking the price
words: 多少, 钱, 块
- 这个多少钱？ 一百块。 | zhège duōshao qián? yì bǎi kuài. | How much is this? 100 dollars.
### Cheap or expensive
太贵了 is a common reaction.
words: 贵, 便宜
- 太贵了！ | tài guì le! | Too expensive!
- 很便宜。 | hěn piányi. | It's cheap.
### Buying
words: 买, 买到, 东西, 衣服, 包, 盒
- 我想买衣服。 | wǒ xiǎng mǎi yīfu. | I want to buy some clothes.
- 我要两盒。 | wǒ yào liǎng hé. | I'll take two boxes.
### Try it
- try: How much is this? | 这个多少钱？ | zhège duōshao qián?
- try: Too expensive! | 太贵了！ | tài guì le!
