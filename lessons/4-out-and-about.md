# Out & about
summary: Places in town, directions, getting around, and shopping.
related: Grammar: Location and "there is"; Grammar: Verbs and helping verbs

## Places in town
words: 商店, 超市, 学校, 公司, 医院, 银行, 公园, 酒店, 机场, 厕所, 北京, 入
- 在 + place = at: 我在超市.
- 去 + place = go to: 我去银行. No 在 after 去.
- 入 is mostly seen on signs (入口, entrance). In speech, use 进.
### Examples
- 厕所在哪里？ | cèsuǒ zài nǎlǐ? | Where's the bathroom?
- 我去超市买东西。 | wǒ qù chāoshì mǎi dōngxi. | I'm going to the supermarket to buy some things.
- 他在医院工作。 | tā zài yīyuàn gōngzuò. | He works at a hospital.
### Try it
- try: Where's the bank? | 银行在哪里？ | yínháng zài nǎlǐ?
- try: I'm going to school. | 我去学校。 | wǒ qù xuéxiào.

## Directions
words: 左边, 右边, 前面, 后面, 前边, 后边, 上, 下, 上面, 下面, 里, 里面, 外面, 旁边, 对面, 中间, 附近, 周围, 角落, 尽头, 对角线, 北, 南, 东, 西, 向上, 向下, 找, 找到, 找不到
- Thing + 在 + place + direction word: 银行在学校旁边.
- The 边 and 面 versions mean the same: 前边 = 前面.
- Short 上 and 里 attach to a noun: 桌子上, 家里.
- 找 = look for. 找到 = found. 找不到 = can't find.
### Examples
- 银行在超市对面。 | yínháng zài chāoshì duìmiàn. | The bank is across from the supermarket.
- 厕所在左边。 | cèsuǒ zài zuǒbiān. | The bathroom is on the left.
- 附近有公园吗？ | fùjìn yǒu gōngyuán ma? | Is there a park nearby?
- 我找不到我的书。 | wǒ zhǎo bu dào wǒ de shū. | I can't find my book.
### Try it
- try: The school is behind the hospital. | 学校在医院后面。 | xuéxiào zài yīyuàn hòumiàn.
- try: I can't find it. | 我找不到。 | wǒ zhǎo bu dào.

## Getting around
words: 来, 去, 走, 回, 到, 坐, 开, 飞机, 出租车, 计程车, 捷运, 公车, 机车, 脚踏车
- 坐 + vehicle = take: 坐捷运, 坐飞机.
- Scooters and bikes use 骑 (qí, ride): 骑机车.
- 开车 = drive a car.
- In Taiwan: 计程车 taxi, 公车 bus, 捷运 MRT.
- 到了 = I'm here / we've arrived.
### Examples
- 我坐捷运去公司。 | wǒ zuò jiéyùn qù gōngsī. | I take the MRT to work.
- 我们坐计程车吧。 | wǒmen zuò jìchéngchē ba. | Let's take a taxi.
- 你什么时候回家？ | nǐ shénme shíhou huí jiā? | When are you going home?
- 我到了！ | wǒ dào le! | I'm here!
### Try it
- try: I take the bus to school. | 我坐公车去学校。 | wǒ zuò gōngchē qù xuéxiào.
- try: Let's go home. | 我们回家吧。 | wǒmen huí jiā ba.

## Shopping and money
words: 买, 买到, 钱, 块, 多少, 东西, 衣服, 包, 盒
- 多少钱？ asks the price.
- 块 is the spoken word for a dollar or yuan: 五十块.
- 买到 = managed to buy.
### Examples
- 这个多少钱？ 一百块。 | zhège duōshao qián? yì bǎi kuài. | How much is this? 100 dollars.
- 我想买衣服。 | wǒ xiǎng mǎi yīfu. | I want to buy some clothes.
- 我要两盒。 | wǒ yào liǎng hé. | I'll take two boxes.
### Try it
- try: How much is this? | 这个多少钱？ | zhège duōshao qián?
- try: I want to buy this. | 我想买这个。 | wǒ xiǎng mǎi zhège.
