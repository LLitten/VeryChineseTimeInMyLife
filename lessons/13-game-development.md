## The game team
Many studios split a team into three parts: 程式 (programming) is the skeleton, 美術 (art) is the skin, and 企劃 (design) is the soul.
### Programming, art and design
Taiwan says 程式 and 企劃; the mainland says 程序 and 策划. Your Taiwanese friends will notice which one you use.
words: 程式, 程序, 美术, 企划, 策划
- 我是游戏美术。 | wǒ shì yóuxì měishù. | I'm a game artist.
- 他是企划。 | tā shì qǐhuà. | He's a game designer.
### Other roles
words: 工程师, 制作人, 设计
- 我是工程师，我写程式。 | wǒ shì gōngchéngshī, wǒ xiě chéngshì. | I'm an engineer. I write code.
- 她是我们的制作人。 | tā shì wǒmen de zhìzuòrén. | She's our producer.
### Try it
- try: I'm a game artist. | 我是游戏美术。 | wǒ shì yóuxì měishù.
- try: What do you do on the team? | 你在团队做什么？ | nǐ zài tuánduì zuò shénme?

## Making a game
### Building it
A game engine (引擎) is the software a game is built in. Taiwan calls software 軟體; the mainland says 软件.
words: 游戏, 开发, 游戏开发, 引擎, 软件
- 你用什么引擎？ | nǐ yòng shénme yǐnqíng? | What engine do you use?
- 我在做游戏开发。 | wǒ zài zuò yóuxì kāifā. | I work in game development.
### Testing and launching
上线 means "go live." After launch, games keep getting 更新 (updates).
words: 测试, 上线, 更新
- 我们下个月上线。 | wǒmen xià ge yuè shàngxiàn. | We launch next month.
- 今天要测试新的关卡。 | jīntiān yào cèshì xīn de guānqiǎ. | Today we're testing the new level.
### Try it
- try: What engine do you use? | 你用什么引擎？ | nǐ yòng shénme yǐnqíng?
- try: We launch next month. | 我们下个月上线。 | wǒmen xià ge yuè shàngxiàn.

## Inside a game
Useful for talking about any game you're playing, not just making one.
### Players and characters
words: 玩家, 角色, 玩游戏
- 你最喜欢哪个角色？ | nǐ zuì xǐhuan nǎge juésè? | Which character is your favorite?
### Levels and saving
words: 关卡, 存档
- 这个关卡太难了！ | zhège guānqiǎ tài nán le! | This level is too hard!
- 别忘了存档。 | bié wàng le cúndàng. | Don't forget to save.
### Try it
- try: This level is too hard! | 这个关卡太难了！ | zhège guānqiǎ tài nán le!

## Game art
Game art usually moves from 2D to 3D: concept art first, then modeling and textures, then animation and effects.
### Concept art
原画 artists design the look of characters and places before anything is built.
words: 原画, 草图, 角色, 设计
- 我在画原画。 | wǒ zài huà yuánhuà. | I'm doing concept art.
- 这是角色的草图。 | zhè shì juésè de cǎotú. | This is a rough sketch of the character.
### 3D modeling and textures
建模 builds the 3D shape; 贴图 paints its surface. In Taiwan, 貼圖 also means LINE stickers.
words: 建模, 贴图
- 我会建模，也会做贴图。 | wǒ huì jiànmó, yě huì zuò tiētú. | I can model, and do textures too.
### Animation and effects
words: 动画, 特效
- 这个特效好漂亮！ | zhège tèxiào hǎo piàoliang! | This effect is so pretty!
### Environments and UI
场景 is the world a game takes place in. The mainland writes UI as 界面; Taiwan writes 介面.
words: 场景, 界面, 颜色
- 我做场景美术。 | wǒ zuò chǎngjǐng měishù. | I'm an environment artist.
### Try it
- try: I'm doing concept art. | 我在画原画。 | wǒ zài huà yuánhuà.
- try: I can do 3D modeling. | 我会建模。 | wǒ huì jiànmó.
