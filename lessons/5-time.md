## Days and dates
Time words go before the verb: 我明天去 (I'm going tomorrow).
### Today, tomorrow, yesterday
天 means “day.”
words: 今天, 明天, 昨天, 现在
- 我今天很忙。 | wǒ jīntiān hěn máng. | I'm busy today.
### Days of the week
星期 + a number: 星期一 is Monday. Sunday is 星期天. Taiwan says xīngqí.
words: 星期, 星期天, 周末
- 今天星期几？ 今天星期五。 | jīntiān xīngqī jǐ? jīntiān xīngqīwǔ. | What day is it? It's Friday.
- 周末你做什么？ | zhōumò nǐ zuò shénme? | What are you doing this weekend?
### Months and dates
Months are number + 月. Dates go big to small: year, month, day.
words: 月, 号, 日, 年, 下个月, 生日
- 今天几月几号？ | jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào? | What's the date today?
- 我的生日是五月三号。 | wǒ de shēngrì shì wǔ yuè sān hào. | My birthday is May 3rd.
### Try it
- try: Tomorrow is Saturday. | 明天星期六。 | míngtiān xīngqīliù.
- try: What's the date today? | 今天几月几号？ | jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào?

## Clock time
### Telling the time
2 o'clock is 两点, not 二点.
words: 点, 分, 时, 秒
- 现在几点？ | xiànzài jǐ diǎn? | What time is it?
- 现在三点二十分。 | xiànzài sān diǎn èrshí fēn. | It's 3:20.
### How long
An hour of time is 小时 (xiǎoshí); 时 alone is formal.
words: 分钟, 时候
- 等我五分钟。 | děng wǒ wǔ fēnzhōng. | Give me five minutes.
### Try it
- try: It's two o'clock. | 现在两点。 | xiànzài liǎng diǎn.

## Parts of the day
Add these before a clock time to be exact: 下午三点 (3 p.m.).
### Morning to night
words: 早上, 上午, 中午, 下午, 晚上
- 我早上七点起床。 | wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng. | I get up at 7 in the morning.
- 我们下午见。 | wǒmen xiàwǔ jiàn. | See you this afternoon.
### Try it
- try: I eat at noon. | 我中午吃饭。 | wǒ zhōngwǔ chīfàn.
source: AllSet: Time words and word order | https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/Time_words_and_word_order

## Seasons and weather
天气怎么样？ asks about the weather. Weather words work like adjectives (很热) or verbs (下雨).
### The four seasons
Each season ends in 天.
words: 春天, 夏天, 秋天, 冬天
- 台湾的夏天很热。 | Táiwān de xiàtiān hěn rè. | Taiwan's summers are hot.
### Hot and cold
words: 天气, 热, 冷, 凉快
- 今天天气怎么样？ 很热！ | jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng? hěn rè! | How's the weather today? Really hot!
### Rain, snow and sun
下 means “to fall.” 了 at the end means it has started: 下雨了.
words: 下雨, 下雪, 刮风, 晴天
- 下雨了。 | xià yǔ le. | It's raining.
- 今天是晴天。 | jīntiān shì qíngtiān. | It's sunny today.
### Try it
- try: It's very cold today. | 今天很冷。 | jīntiān hěn lěng.
source: LTL: Days, months and seasons in Chinese | https://ltl-singapore.com/days-months-seasons-in-chinese/

## Making plans
### Suggesting
吧 turns a sentence into a suggestion.
words: 走吧, 一起去吧, 电影
- 明天一起去看电影吧！ | míngtiān yìqǐ qù kàn diànyǐng ba! | Let's go see a movie tomorrow!
### Saying yes
words: 好啊, 可以, 等一下, 什么时候
- 星期六可以吗？ 可以！ | xīngqīliù kěyǐ ma? kěyǐ! | Does Saturday work? Yes!
### Try it
- try: Let's go! | 走吧！ | zǒu ba!
- try: Let's go together. | 一起去吧。 | yìqǐ qù ba.
