# Feelings
summary: Saying how you feel, and asking how others feel.
intro: Talking about how you feel makes conversations with friends more personal. Feelings work like other describing words, so the patterns you already know apply here too.
related: Conversation; Body & health; Grammar: Adverbs and describing

## How you feel
Feelings work like other describing words: 我很开心 (I'm happy). Add 不太 for "not very," or 太…了 for "so."
### Good feelings
开心 is casual; 高兴 is a bit more formal.
words: 开心, 高兴, 兴奋
- 今天我很开心！ | jīntiān wǒ hěn kāixīn! | I'm really happy today!
- 我好兴奋！ | wǒ hǎo xīngfèn! | I'm so excited!
### Bad feelings
words: 难过, 生气, 害怕, 失望
- 你生气了吗？ | nǐ shēngqì le ma? | Are you angry?
- 我不太难过。 | wǒ bú tài nánguò. | I'm not too sad.
### Nervous and worried
words: 紧张, 担心, 别担心
- 我很紧张。 | wǒ hěn jǐnzhāng. | I'm nervous.
- 别担心！ | bié dānxīn! | Don't worry!
### Bored, tired, awkward
无聊 means both “bored” and “boring.”
words: 无聊, 累, 尴尬, 想家
- 好无聊！ | hǎo wúliáo! | So boring!
- 好尴尬。 | hǎo gāngà. | So awkward.
### Try it
- try: I'm so happy! | 我好开心！ | wǒ hǎo kāixīn!
- try: Are you nervous? | 你紧张吗？ | nǐ jǐnzhāng ma?
source: LTL: Feelings in Chinese | https://ltl-singapore.com/feelings-in-chinese/

## Asking how someone feels
### Checking in
你还好吗？ means “are you okay?”
words: 怎么了？, 没事
- 你怎么了？ 我有点累。 | nǐ zěnme le? wǒ yǒudiǎn lèi. | What's wrong? I'm a bit tired.
- 有点 (yǒudiǎn) + feeling = "a bit," usually for negative feelings.
### Try it
- try: What's wrong? | 怎么了？ | zěnme le?
