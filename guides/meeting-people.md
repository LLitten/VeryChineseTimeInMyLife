# Meeting people & small talk
related: People, Conversation, Grammar: Yes / no and other questions
summary: Saying hi, introducing yourself, and keeping a casual chat going with friends.

Written by Claude, not from the handwritten notes. The phrases are everyday, friendly Mandarin as heard in Taiwan; check anything that sounds off with friends.

## Saying hi
Between friends, a simple 嗨 or 你好 is enough. 你好吗？ is correct but sounds like a textbook; friends usually ask how things have been.
- 嗨！ | hāi! | Hi!
- 好久不见！ | hǎojiǔ bújiàn! | Long time no see!
- 最近怎么样？ | zuìjìn zěnmeyàng? | How have you been?
- 还好。 | hái hǎo. | Okay, not bad. | The most common answer.
- 不错！ | búcuò! | Pretty good!
- 吃饭了吗？ | chīfàn le ma? | Have you eaten? | A friendly greeting, not always an invitation.

## Introducing yourself
- 我叫… | wǒ jiào… | My name is…
- 你叫什么名字？ | nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? | What's your name?
- 我是美国人。 | wǒ shì Měiguó rén. | I'm American.
- 你是哪里人？ | nǐ shì nǎlǐ rén? | Where are you from?
- 我在学中文。 | wǒ zài xué Zhōngwén. | I'm learning Chinese.
- 我的中文不太好。 | wǒ de Zhōngwén bú tài hǎo. | My Chinese isn't very good.
- 很高兴认识你。 | hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ. | Nice to meet you. | A little formal between young friends.

## Keeping the chat going
Short reactions show you're listening. Asking the question back with 你呢？ keeps things moving.
- 你呢？ | nǐ ne? | And you?
- 真的吗？ | zhēn de ma? | Really?
- 是吗？ | shì ma? | Oh, is that so? | Milder than 真的吗？
- 然后呢？ | ránhòu ne? | And then?
- 对啊！ | duì a! | Yeah, right!
- 我也是。 | wǒ yě shì. | Me too.
- 好厉害！ | hǎo lìhai! | Wow, impressive!
- 你做什么工作？ | nǐ zuò shénme gōngzuò? | What do you do for work?
- 你喜欢做什么？ | nǐ xǐhuan zuò shénme? | What do you like to do?

## When you get stuck
- 什么意思？ | shénme yìsi? | What does that mean?
- 请说慢一点。 | qǐng shuō màn yìdiǎn. | Please speak a bit slower.
- 这个怎么说？ | zhège zěnme shuō? | How do you say this?
- 听不懂。 | tīng bu dǒng. | I didn't understand.

## Saying bye
- 我先走了。 | wǒ xiān zǒu le. | I'm heading out. | Polite when you leave before the others.
- 我们加LINE吧！ | wǒmen jiā LINE ba! | Let's add each other on LINE! | LINE is the main messaging app in Taiwan.
- 下次见！ | xià cì jiàn! | See you next time!
- 掰掰！ | bāibāi! | Bye! | Often written 拜拜 on the mainland.

## Example dialogue
Two friends run into each other. B brings a friend, C, who is from Taipei.
- A: 嗨！好久不见！ | hāi! hǎojiǔ bújiàn! | Hi! Long time no see!
- B: 好久不见！最近怎么样？ | hǎojiǔ bújiàn! zuìjìn zěnmeyàng? | Long time no see! How have you been?
- A: 还不错。你呢？ | hái búcuò. nǐ ne? | Pretty good. And you?
- B: 我很好。这是我的朋友，她叫美美。 | wǒ hěn hǎo. zhè shì wǒ de péngyou, tā jiào Měiměi. | I'm good. This is my friend, her name is Meimei.
- C: 你好！ | nǐ hǎo! | Hi!
- A: 你好！你是哪里人？ | nǐ hǎo! nǐ shì nǎlǐ rén? | Hi! Where are you from?
- C: 我是台北人。你呢？ | wǒ shì Táiběi rén. nǐ ne? | I'm from Taipei. And you?
- A: 我是美国人。我在学中文，可是我的中文不太好。 | wǒ shì Měiguó rén. wǒ zài xué Zhōngwén, kěshì wǒ de Zhōngwén bú tài hǎo. | I'm American. I'm learning Chinese, but my Chinese isn't very good.
- C: 不会啊，你说得很好！ | bú huì a, nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo! | Not at all, you speak really well!
- A: 没有啦！ | méiyǒu la! | Oh, not really!
- B: 我们加LINE吧！ | wǒmen jiā LINE ba! | Let's add each other on LINE!

## Good to know
- **Answering praise:** people usually brush compliments off. 没有啦 or 还好啦 (啦 softens the sentence) are common in Taiwan. 哪里哪里 is the textbook answer and sounds old-fashioned.
- **说得很好** uses 得 to describe how well something is done (verb + 得 + adjective). It's beyond A1, but you'll hear it a lot.
- **可是** means "but." It's a step past the A1 list, and very common.
- **台北人, 美国人:** a place + 人 means a person from there.
